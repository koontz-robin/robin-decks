01 · Why evaluate now

A growing, multi-service business needs more visibility—and fewer systems holding the workflow together.

Owner-level visibility

Service Fusion reporting does not give leadership the accessible operational view needed to run and scale the business confidently.

Complex work

New construction, retrofits, and multi-phase projects are managed outside the PSA because the current project workflow is too limited.

Growth into new services

Networking and VoIP are joining access control, cameras, alarms, doors, low voltage, and locksmithing—raising the value of one flexible platform.

Evaluation thesis: The best-fit platform should simplify today’s operations while giving A&B room to add services without adding another layer of disconnected tools.

02 · Current operating model

Core systems work—but estimates, projects, inventory, and recurring billing still require handoffs.

Current stack

Service FusionQuickBooksClickUpZapierCentral Alarm

Service Fusion handles tickets, quotes, and invoices; QuickBooks remains the accounting ledger; ClickUp tracks estimate stages and project phases; Zapier connects the pieces.

Where friction shows up

  • Reporting is difficult to turn into useful management visibility.
  • Annual and agreement-based billing is cumbersome.
  • Project phases and estimate follow-up live in ClickUp.
  • Inventory is a product list—not live stock across offices and trucks.
  • Customer communications require more automation and targeting.
Current stateService Fusion + ClickUp + QuickBooks
03 · Target future state

A connected workflow built around the way A&B actually sells, installs, services, and bills.

Estimate to project

Track opportunities and quote status, capture e-signatures, and convert won work into structured project phases without recreating the job in another tool.

Dispatch to completion

Schedule field teams, track travel and work time, send push notifications, capture notes and photos, and keep technicians productive from mobile.

Parts to profitability

Know what is available by office or truck, reserve material for work, connect purchasing to jobs, and preserve a clear asset and cost trail.

Agreement to invoice

Support recurring and annual billing for alarm, access-control, and service agreements while keeping QuickBooks focused on accounting.

Data to decisions

Give Brian and the management team clear reporting across sales, service, projects, inventory, billing, and profitability.

One customer experience

Automate appointment reminders, technician ETA messages, and targeted outreach while preserving a responsive, local service model.

05 · Illustrative ROI framework

Small gains across a field-heavy team can create meaningful monthly value.

Field efficiency

~$2,440 per month

With approximately 15 field technicians, saving just 10 minutes per technician per workday returns about 54 hours monthly. At an illustrative $45 loaded hourly cost, that is roughly $2,440 in recovered capacity.

Office efficiency

~$585 per month

If three office or project users each recover only one hour per week from reporting, status updates, billing handoffs, and ClickUp coordination, that returns about 13 hours monthly at the same illustrative labor value.

Billing capture

~$650 per month

Capturing just one additional billable hour per week through better mobile time entry, project tracking, and agreement billing produces about $650 monthly at an illustrative $150 billable rate.

$3,675 monthly potential

The three conservative scenarios total approximately $3,675 per month, or about $44,100 annually, before software consolidation or inventory improvements.

Uncounted upside

Retiring or reducing Service Fusion, ClickUp, and Zapier costs; avoiding duplicate entry; improving purchasing; accelerating invoicing; and reducing stock loss are not included in the quantified case.

Inputs to validate

Confirm active field and office users, loaded labor cost, billable rate, software spend, reporting hours, billing leakage, inventory adjustments, and project-delay costs before finalizing ROI.

Conservative takeaway: The value case does not require a dramatic transformation. Ten minutes per field technician per day, one office hour per week, and one recovered billable hour per week create a meaningful hurdle for the investment to clear.

Illustrative model only—not a quote or guaranteed savings. Assumptions: 15 field technicians, three office/project users, 21.7 workdays per month, $45 loaded labor cost, and $150 billable rate. Final ROI should use A&B’s actual operating and commercial data.
