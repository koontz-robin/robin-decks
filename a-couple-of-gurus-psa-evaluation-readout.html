2. Why make the change now?

Growth in managed services makes the PSA the operating backbone, not just the ticket queue.

When service delivery, documentation, contracts, billing handoff, and customer visibility live in separate workflows, every new client adds avoidable administrative drag. Rev.io PSA is positioned to bring the operating motion closer to revenue capture.

The strategic question: can A Couple of Gurus run service, projects, recurring agreements, reporting, and billing handoff from one cleaner workflow before process complexity scales further?

More clients, more handoffs

Recurring MSP work creates repeated touchpoints: alerts, tickets, dispatch, time, parts, project work, approvals, invoices, and account reviews.

More compliance context

Security and compliance-oriented clients need stronger proof of what was done, when it was completed, and how recurring obligations were handled.

More revenue leakage risk

Manual handoff from service work to billing can delay invoicing, miss billable time, or make profitability harder to inspect by client and contract.

3. Uncovered problems

The evaluation should focus on where service work leaks time, context, and margin.

Operational friction to confirm

  • Where technicians lose time updating tickets, notes, time entries, or project tasks.
  • Where dispatch and service visibility are slower than leadership needs.
  • Which workflows leave the PSA for spreadsheets, emails, accounting tools, or separate portals.

Financial friction to confirm

  • Whether billable time, recurring services, add-ons, and project work are captured consistently.
  • How much manual review is needed before invoices are ready.
  • Which clients or agreement types are hardest to inspect for margin.

Technical fit to confirm

  • Current PSA/RMM/documentation/accounting stack and required integrations.
  • Data migration requirements for clients, contacts, assets, agreements, tickets, projects, and time history.
  • Reporting and dashboard needs by service manager, technician, owner, and finance user.

Customer experience to confirm

  • Portal, communication, approval, and status visibility expectations.
  • How client-facing documentation is handled today.
  • Where response, resolution, and follow-up metrics need to improve.
