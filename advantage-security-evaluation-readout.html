2. Business issue

Manual handoffs are consuming leadership time and hiding margin until it is too late to act.

Billing takes a full day

Recurring billing takes more than seven hours on the first of each month. Installation and recurring charges must be separated manually, creating interruption risk, cadence errors, and missed revenue.

Profitability requires a calculator

Per-item margin is checked manually in QuickBooks Online. Actual labor is reconciled later, so quoted-versus-actual performance and the reason for margin erosion are difficult to see in time.

Inventory lives on a clipboard

Truck and shop stock are tracked manually. The team cannot reliably answer what is available, where it is, what job needs it, or whether reserved material has been billed.

Field notes need cleanup

The operations manager reviews and rewrites technician notes before they reach customers—adding administrative effort and creating avoidable quality risk.

Scheduling is fragmented

QuickBooks/Intuit scheduling does not reliably synchronize with Outlook. OneNote, Outlook, QuickBooks Online, and manual timesheets divide the operating record.

KPI visibility is missing

Service-call volume, technician utilization, labor variance, and job profitability require spreadsheet work rather than appearing as live, role-secured operating measures.

3. Future-state workflow

One operational thread from quote approval to installation, recurring service, accounting, and performance review.

01

Quote

Build installation and recurring elements together, with line-item and total margin visible before approval.

02

Plan

Convert approved work into tickets, reserve parts, generate vendor POs, and schedule the right technician.

03

Execute

Use mobile time logs, notes, attachments, and customer signatures while maintaining a complete job record.

04

Bill

Invoice installation work and activate recurring charges at the intended cadence without rebuilding the sale.

05

Learn

Compare quoted versus actual labor and margin; monitor service volume and exceptions with live analytics.

QuickBooks Online remains the accounting system. Rev.io synchronizes matched customers, products, invoices, and purchase orders one-for-one to reduce double entry and preserve the financial record.

5. Business impact

The clearest return begins with recovered time, protected recurring revenue, and earlier margin intervention.

Return leadership time

Automating recurring billing targets the seven-plus-hour monthly process directly—while reducing the concentration risk of one person managing every cadence and exception.

Protect recurring revenue

Recurring services can activate from completed installation work instead of relying on OneNote, memory, or a future manual reminder.

Protect project margin

Visible quote margin, actual labor cost, material changes, and threshold alerts move profitability review from after-the-fact reconciliation into daily operations.

Increase operating accountability

Technician time, service-call volume, inventory movement, signatures, notes, and billing status become measurable in one workflow.

ROI discipline: This readout intentionally avoids inventing a dollar return. A final business case should use Advantage Security’s actual loaded labor cost, billing exceptions, missed recurring revenue, inventory adjustments, and current software spend.
