01 · Business issue

Growth is being supported by capable people—but too many workflows still depend on paper, disconnected tools, and owner knowledge.

Fragmented operations

Job details, photos, hours, and documents live across QuickBooks Desktop, Outlook, shared drives, scanned files, and paper work orders.

Owner dependency

Mike remains deeply involved in routine details. Better visibility and delegation would let the team answer more questions without routing everything through the owner.

Cost-sensitive change

Any new platform must justify its investment, preserve accounting and recurring payments, and be practical for a small team with varied technology comfort.

Evaluation thesis: The right platform should create one operating view without forcing ASI to trade away the accounting, payment, and customer workflows it relies on today.
Why changeDiscovery + demonstrations · October–November 2026
02 · Current friction

Manual work appears at every handoff—from quote creation to cash collection.

Quoting

Word-based proposals take time and need better product information, attachments, signatures, and support for Hunter Douglas documentation.

Projects and deposits

Construction jobs can run 8–12 months. Fifty-percent deposits are tracked on paper, and an unpaid deposit can remain unnoticed for a month.

Collections

A dedicated employee spends significant time on collections while customer messaging and escalation rules vary by account and job.

Recurring billing

Heather manually audits Alarm.com and Becklar charges against customer billing to identify missed or incorrect recurring revenue.

Tax administration

Rate updates consume several hours a week leading into monthly invoicing; the true cycle and savings opportunity should be baselined.

Migration and adoption

ASI wants to preserve automatic payment reconciliation, avoid re-entering 100+ cards, and prevent a repeat of earlier accounting and inventory friction.

Current statePaper + QuickBooks Desktop + Outlook + shared files

04 · Value to validate

The value case should be built from measurable operating improvements—not generic software promises.

100+

Recurring cards protected

Confirm whether token portability can avoid manual re-entry and prevent customer disruption during migration.

4–5

Expected field users

Validate the daily mobile workflow, connectivity, training needs, and whether a fifth field license is required.

8–12 mo.

Long project lifecycle

Prove that deposits, milestones, labor, material, progress billing, retainage, and job visibility work for representative construction projects.

Working-capital improvement

Measure current time from deposit request to receipt, overdue balances, collection effort, and the value of faster escalation.

Recovered team capacity

Baseline time spent building proposals, locating job information, reconciling vendor charges, updating taxes, and answering routine status questions.

Revenue assurance

Test real Alarm.com and Becklar files to quantify underbilling, overbilling, review effort, and correction workflows using ASI data.

Payment economics

Compare three merchant statements with a formal Rev.io quote across ASI’s estimated $30,000–$50,000 monthly card volume, including fees passed to customers. No processing savings are assumed yet.

Software consolidation

Validate every proposal and signature workflow, then compare actual PandaDoc and DocuSign costs and renewal dates before treating them as removable spend.

Owner capacity

Measure Mike’s routine coordination hours and realistically redeployable time without assuming additional sales, profit, or headcount reduction.

Important boundary: Heather’s estimate that broader automation and delegation might save up to half of a salary is a hypothesis to validate—not a guaranteed or reconciliation-only saving.

05 · Proof plan

Use ASI scenarios to convert product fit into decision-quality evidence.

Quote and project proof

  • Build a real Hunter Douglas quote with required documents and signatures.
  • Run one representative long construction project and one short security service job.
  • Confirm whether PandaDoc or DocuSign can be retired.

Billing and collections proof

  • Reproduce ASI’s 50% deposit, milestone, final invoice, and retainage flows.
  • Configure desired customer communication and escalation rules.
  • Review merchant statements and confirm payment-token portability.

Accounting and revenue proof

  • Test QuickBooks categories, sync, reconciliation, and tax scenarios.
  • Load actual Alarm.com and Becklar files and confirm product matching.
  • Measure the review and correction workflow using ASI examples.

Adoption proof

  • Validate technician schedules, notes, photos, time, and offline use.
  • Confirm devices, connectivity, user count, and training approach.
  • Define which employee records remain in a separate HR solution.
