### Project Management Demo
      - Projects can be created at any point in the deal lifecycle, or duplicated from templates — all phases, tasks, to-dos, milestones, and tickets carry over  
      - Projects are structured around phases (e.g., site assessment, design, procurement, installation, testing) with milestones and tickets nested inside 
      - Ticket time rolls up to phase-level budgets, then to overall project budget, enabling early detection of labor overruns 
      - Gantt chart with drag-and-drop scheduling is available (day/week/month views); changes auto-sync to the table view 
      - Activity feed provides a real-time log of all ticket updates, notes, and phase changes 
      - Prospect noted project management is comparable to both ConnectWise and Halo; drag-and-drop Gantt was highlighted as a differentiator 

### Project Billing
      - Billing options from a single project view: deposit/prepayment invoices, milestone invoices, and true progressive invoicing  
      - Progressive invoicing allows line-item selection (e.g., bill all materials but no labor, or 50% of hardware) and honors Rev.io's automated tax engine  
      - Tax engine applies taxes per jurisdiction and per product tax code — three variables: product mapping, billed amount, and service location 
      - Prospect noted ConnectWise limitation: services are set globally as non-taxable, with no ability to override individual service products (a compliance issue in NY where physical labor components should be taxable)  
      - In Rev.io, all billing items are products with individual tax codes, providing full granularity 

### Agreements
      - Agreements support a start date, initial term end date, and expiration date as three separate fields; agreements without an expiration are "evergreen" and continue billing until canceled 
      - Rate increases can be scheduled in advance and set to take effect on a specific future date (e.g., renewal date) 
      - Key innovation: Components — multiple billing rules (monthly, quarterly, annual reoccurring services; block hour pools; time & materials) can coexist within a single agreement, unlike ConnectWise which requires multiple agreements   
      - Block hour pools support tiered overage billing (e.g., hours 11–20 at $75/hr, 21+ at $100/hr) and multiple pools (e.g., onsite vs. remote hours)  
      - Agreement tickets can be filtered by ticket type — noted as potentially useful for co-managed scoping  
      - Agreement profitability tracked month-over-month with a rolling 4-month margin trend view  
      - Prospect asked about using two agreements (services vs. subscriptions) to separate profitability views — confirmed possible, and both can still roll into one invoice  
      - Margin-threshold workflow triggers not yet available natively, but Revy can run scheduled reports to flag agreements below a set margin  

### Revy AI Features
      - Revy automations: any query can be scheduled to run and deliver proactive reports (e.g., weekly margin alerts) 
      - Skills workshop: pre-built and custom "skills" (elaborate prompts) let users run real-time reports without SQL knowledge — Revy can write the SQL  
      - MCP servers coming by Q4 
      - Teams integration for tagging Revy in chat announced at Rev.io's recent summit

## Value Planner - Altourage

## Business Issue
  Altourage is evaluating a PSA migration away from ConnectWise Manage after running it since 2020. The team is actively comparing Halo PSA and Rev.io PSA, with a firm platform decision expected in Q4 and migration beginning in Q1 next year. Their current ConnectWise environment creates operational friction across ticketing, quoting, integrations, asset deduplication, reporting, and customer/co-managed support. They need a modern PSA that can support a 75-user MSP operation, reduce duplicate systems/workflows, and give leadership confidence that migration risk, onboarding effort, and roadmap dependencies are manageable.

## Problems
  - ConnectWise is viewed as dated and creates pain around duplicate asset records across tools like NinjaOne, Huntress, ITGlue, and ScalePad.
  - The team relies heavily on chat-native support workflows, currently generating roughly 100–150 chat-native tickets/month; Rev.io’s current Teams/Slack capabilities are workflow-trigger based, not full Thread-style chat-native ticket handling.
  - Business Central integration is a hard requirement for the Lyra portfolio and must be available before Altourage can seriously proceed.
  - Co-managed/customer portal capabilities are important because Altourage needs client users to submit/work tickets, access their own dashboards, and remain properly permissioned/siloed.
  - Quote approval workflows are not yet built; Altourage asked for threshold-based approvals such as equipment quotes over $10K.
  - They need clarity on agreements, block hours, project management, integrations, permissions, and onboarding cost comparison versus Halo.

## Value
  Rev.io can win by positioning itself as a more modern, integrated PSA platform with strong UI, native quoting, AI, reporting, mobile, onboarding, and data migration advantages. Altourage rated Rev.io’s UI strongest compared with ConnectWise, Autotask, and Halo. Rev.io’s native quote/catalog/distributor workflow can reduce sync and duplication issues from tools like Quoter. Built-in analytics, SQL access, and Revy can replace or reduce dependence on BrightGauge-style reporting. Dedicated onboarding, included migration resources, and API-based ConnectWise migration lower implementation risk versus outsourced Halo migration. If Rev.io delivers or clearly validates Business Central, co-managed portal, and chat roadmap needs by Q4, Altourage stated Rev.io remains on the shortlist.

## Power
  Primary evaluators/decision-makers are Matt/Matthew and Allison, with Allison tied to finance/HR and broader team stakeholders expected to join later demos. Matthew is Director of Support and appears to be driving operational evaluation and technical fit. Allison is expected to validate financial/operational requirements. A broader Lyra influence exists: multiple Lyra opcos are exploring Rev.io PSA, Lyra Communications is already a Rev.io billing customer, and Rev.io is pursuing a formal Lyra group deal. A Lyra-level deal could create a “stamp of approval” and reduce individual opco negotiation friction.

## Plan
  - Send a follow-up summary with proposed times for the next call and include Allison.
  - Before the next call, prepare a current-state update on co-managed portal capabilities and roadmap timing.
  - Log and track the feature request to display SLA response timers on the dispatch board.
  - Confirm and communicate the current Business Central integration status / launch timing.
  - Prepare the next demo around Altourage’s open evaluation areas: agreements and block hours, project management, co-managed portal, integration library, permissions model, and onboarding cost breakdown.
  - Address quoting approval gaps honestly while showing current workflow/to-do alternatives and roadmap direction.
  - Continue positioning Rev.io against Halo by emphasizing lower migration dependency, included onboarding resources, UI strength, native catalog/quoting, reporting depth, and month-to-month flexibility.

## Risk / Mitigation Plan
  Risk: Halo remains a strong competitor and Altourage has already invested time in Halo evaluation and migration planning.  
  Mitigation: Differentiate Rev.io on UI, onboarding model, API-based migration, native quoting/catalog, reporting, AI, and Lyra group alignment.
  Risk: Business Central, co-managed portal, and chat-native ticketing are gating requirements for Q4 consideration.  
  Mitigation: Provide specific roadmap/current-status updates, identify what is live vs. in development, and offer to bring product/SE resources into the next call.
  Risk: Rev.io lacks formal quote approval workflows today.  
  Mitigation: Show available workflow notifications/to-dos, confirm threshold approval direction is aligned with roadmap, and document Altourage’s specific requirement.
  Risk: Onboarding cost may look higher than Halo’s initial estimate.  
  Mitigation: Reframe around total cost and risk: Rev.io’s flat onboarding fee includes data migration, dedicated onboarding consultant, migration engineer, and continued AE involvement, while Halo requires external migration support over 9–12 months.
  Risk: Broader buying committee has not fully engaged yet.  
  Mitigation: Include Allison and additional stakeholders in the next call, tailor demo content to financial, operational, and technical decision criteria, and create a concise leadership narrative around why Rev.io is a safer PSA migration path.
