3. Today’s validation · Projects, billing, and tax

Rev.io demonstrated mature project controls and billing flexibility—with a meaningful tax-compliance advantage.

Reusable project execution

Create projects at any deal stage or duplicate templates with phases, tasks, to-dos, milestones, and working tickets. The documents area links original and change quotes; ticket time rolls into phase and total labor budgets.

Planning and visibility

Day, week, and month Gantt views support drag-and-drop scheduling, while dates can also be edited inline. Activity and role-restricted profitability views expose execution status, margin, and remaining billable value.

Three billing paths

Use deposits/prepayments, one or more milestones followed by a balance invoice, or true progressive invoicing from attached ticket and quote line items. Paid deposits and milestones deduct from the final invoice.

Granular tax treatment

Progressive invoices preserve item-level tax treatment; deposits and milestones are generally untaxed. Distinct products/reason codes can separate taxable physical labor from non-taxable configuration work by jurisdiction.

ConnectWise gap addressed

Altourage cannot currently override service taxability below a global ConnectWise setting, creating a New York compliance concern. Rev.io can separate reason codes and billing products with distinct tax treatment.

Customer reaction

Matt described the project model as comparable to both ConnectWise and Halo; the drag-and-drop Gantt experience was specifically called out as valuable.

4. Today’s validation · Agreements

One agreement can support the billing and service rules that require multiple records in ConnectWise.

Lifecycle controls

Separate start, initial-term-end, and expiration dates support renewal notifications and evergreen agreements. Changes can take effect today, next cycle/period, or on any future date, with proration where applicable.

Components model

Monthly, quarterly, annual, unlimited coverage, included-hour pools, tiered overages, and time-and-material rules can coexist inside one agreement—without separate records for every cadence.

Flexible hour pools

Create multiple pools such as onsite and remote, then apply tiered overage rates—for example, hours 11–20 at one rate and 21+ at another.

Coverage controls

Limit agreement coverage by ticket type and linked assets, which may become useful for scoping co-managed responsibilities.

Profitability visibility

Track a rolling four-month margin trend and drill into the underlying components, time types, sources, and logged activity. Separate service and subscription agreements can still combine onto one invoice.

Margin automation path

Native margin-threshold workflow triggers are not yet available. Today, Revii can schedule recurring reports to flag agreements below a selected margin; Product will evaluate a direct workflow trigger.

6. Co-managed operating model

Co-managed capability is a strategic sales differentiator—not simply a portal checkbox.

Altourage’s current model

Clients generally keep a small internal IT team and their own ticketing system while Altourage owns infrastructure/back-end work and the customer relationship. Today Altourage has fewer than 10–20 co-managed clients.

Required client experience

Users must be restricted to their company, with configurable company-wide versus submitter-only ticket visibility, appropriate notes, and collaboration. Product discussed this direction; it was not demonstrated and still needs validation.

Complex edge cases

Some clients maintain their own ticketing systems, making bidirectional synchronization difficult because of email loops. Scoped third-party vendor access may matter later, but is not a day-one requirement.

Product collaboration

Rev.io Product is actively designing co-managed functionality with large onboarding clients. Serge requested Altourage’s current-state and ideal-state workflow map to inform the design and next dedicated session.

Project collaboration gap

A client-facing project workspace is not available today. Altourage wants shared budgets, timelines, tasks, and mutual assignment; Moovila, monday.com, and Asana were referenced as comparators.

Why it matters

Even at today’s client count, co-managed delivery helps Altourage win and retain larger agreements. The evaluation must confirm scope, permissions, notes, collaboration, and future vendor participation.
