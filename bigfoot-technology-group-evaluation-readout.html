05 · ROI framework

The investment can pay for itself with a small improvement in utilization—or one additional billable hour per week.

Ramp scenario

$533 monthly gap

Rev.io PSA ($1,130) plus a $500 telecom ramp totals $1,630/month. Compared with today’s $597 PSA spend plus the $500 already available for telecom billing, the incremental investment is $533/month.

Break-even

6.7 hours per month

At an illustrative $80/hour loaded labor value, saving about 1.5 hours per week across the team covers the $533 monthly gap.

Revenue case

1 billable hour per week

At an illustrative $150 billable rate, recovering one additional billable hour each week produces about $650/month—enough to offset the ramp-period gap.

Tool consolidation

Replacing ConnectWise Manage, CPQ/Sell, BrightGauge, and potentially the separate time clock creates additional savings not included in the break-even math.

Fewer billing handoffs

Automated recurring services, one-for-one QuickBooks sync, agreement billing, and consolidated invoices reduce manual entry and missed-charge risk.

Voice growth upside

Bringing approximately $2,000/month of voice billing in-house establishes the operating foundation to retain more economics as direct voice revenue grows.

Conservative takeaway: The quantified hurdle is only $533 per month during the proposed ramp. This model excludes software licenses retired, reduced consultant dependence, faster collections, fewer billing errors, and future voice-margin expansion.

Illustrative ROI uses the pricing discussed on October 2 and assumed labor/billable rates. Final ROI should be updated with Bigfoot’s actual loaded labor rate, customer billable rate, retired software costs, and revised proposal.
