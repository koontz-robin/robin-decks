01 · Why change now

A renewal deadline turns known friction into an immediate platform decision.

Security-driven reset

Following a security incident, Bigfoot wants to replace ConnectWise Manage, CPQ/Sell, and RMM rather than recommit to the incumbent stack.

Renewal pressure

Renewal is expected around October 1. The team needs a credible path quickly, while a full implementation is expected to take 2–3 months.

Revenue control

Bigfoot wants to move from agent to direct telecom biller, own customer billing, and capture usage revenue in-house.

Executive thesis: consolidate the service-to-cash workflow now, without trading urgency for unmanaged implementation, compliance, or carrier risk.

06 · Risk landscape

Move quickly—but make every dependency explicit.

Renewal compression

Risk: an imminent ConnectWise date forces a status-quo renewal.
Response: establish a renewal bridge and dated 2–3 month implementation plan.

Roadmap fit

Risk: MCP is roadmap-dependent and there is no native GoHighLevel integration.
Response: distinguish available APIs/workarounds from committed product capability.

Compliance ownership

Risk: tax filing and broader telecom compliance responsibilities are unclear.
Response: map partner, Bigfoot, and Rev.io ownership in writing.

Carrier coverage

Risk: rural rate-center carrier limits constrain the direct-biller model.
Response: validate footprint, exceptions, and fallback paths.

Commercial uncertainty

Risk: telecom pricing is unconfirmed and payments/term are open.
Response: deliver a complete, assumption-labeled quote.

Unmet management

Risk: the final decision group has not been engaged.
Response: bring management into the evaluation before asking for commitment.
