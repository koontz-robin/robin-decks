1. Who is BSN Security?

A local security integrator selling trust, documentation, and response speed.

Greater Phoenix footprint

BSN serves Scottsdale, Phoenix, Mesa, Tucson, and surrounding Arizona markets with surveillance, gate systems, access control, visitor management, and guard services.

Commercial buyer profile

The Value Planner centers on HOAs and property managers: buyers who need a professional proposal, site-level documentation, and confidence that support will not disappear after install.

Recurring revenue model

BSN wants to protect margin on monitoring and video verification while packaging equipment, service, and ongoing charges into clean monthly lease options.

The buying experience is part of the product. If the proposal, portal, PM schedule, and service history feel fragmented, BSN's premium promise gets harder to defend.

2. Why make the change now?

The sales model is getting more sophisticated than the current tool stack.

Commercial credibility is on the line

Jobber proposals do not create the premium, storyboard-style experience BSN wants for larger commercial stakeholders. The more complex the site, the more the proposal needs to carry the value story.

Leasing adds operational pressure

Purchase vs. lease choices, 36/48/60-month terms, bundled monthly service, and equipment details need structured quoting and contract logic instead of manual management.

PM work cannot live in spreadsheets

Preventative maintenance scheduling is currently exposed to missed steps, owner dependency, and manual tracking. That risk compounds as BSN expands recurring service obligations.

Video verification margin needs control

Inconsistent CMS central-station pricing creates immediate margin risk. BSN needs clearer package tracking, profitability visibility, and operating consistency per camera/site.

3. Uncovered problems

The issues are not isolated. They connect from proposal to billing to service proof.

Sales presentation

  • Proposal output does not feel differentiated enough for commercial deals.
  • Storyboard selling and visual/site sections are difficult to represent cleanly.
  • Purchase vs. lease options require manual workarounds.

Revenue operations

  • Lease terms and bundled monthly charges need better contract visibility.
  • Central-station pricing inconsistency pressures monitoring and video verification margins.
  • Catalog items, kits, and repeatable templates are needed to reduce quote variation.

Service delivery

  • PM schedules are tracked in spreadsheets.
  • Reports, project photos, videos, and history are emailed and get lost.
  • Customers need a stronger portal experience for ticket and project documentation.
