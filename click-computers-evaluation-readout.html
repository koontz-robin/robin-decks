1. Business context

A hometown IT team with two businesses under one owner—and two Odoo instances.

Local, trusted service

Click Computers has served Central Texas since 2004 with computer repair, technology consulting, cybersecurity, backups, and managed IT support—backed by a 4.9-star Google rating from 204 reviews shown on its website.

Mixed customer model

The business combines recurring MSP services with a residential storefront that represents roughly 30% of the operation, creating both scheduled and transactional workflows.

Recent operating change

Owner Tyler Andrews acquired Click and moved the MSP onto a second Odoo instance used alongside PoE Texas. Dalton is now evaluating whether a purpose-built platform can simplify the model.

2. Why change now?

Routine work is becoming backend configuration, manual matching, and rework.

Quoting is fragile

New products require backend setup and tax coding. To avoid broken invoices, staff reuse a generic “MSP hardware” item and manually edit descriptions and pricing.

Tax exceptions consume the day

A ten-minute task can become an hour when tax setup fails. Dalton described recurring friction that can surface as often as every other day.

Alerts need human translation

The custom NinjaOne integration is one-way. Alerts arrive as raw text and staff manually identify the correct customer across roughly 60–100 tickets each week.

Billing remains hands-on

Staff review monthly billing, check payments daily, handle failed tokens and checks, and follow up on collections—while ticket notes and billable work remain difficult to connect.

5. What Rev.io must prove

Use Click's real products, alerts, customers, and payment exceptions.

Quote, product, and tax

  • Create a new product on the fly and apply the correct tax treatment by product type and address.
  • Use price books, bundles, macros, options, proposal templates, and e-signature.
  • Validate Click's edge cases—not a clean demo catalog.

NinjaOne and service

  • Prove customer and asset sync, alert association, routing, and exceptions.
  • Show labor, parts, entitlements, notes, and customer history on the ticket.
  • Measure actual match rate and time saved.

Billing and payments

  • Trace one-time and recurring quote items into service and billing.
  • Validate first-of-month runs, proration, autopay, failed tokens, checks, and collection follow-up.
  • Define TaxCloud, accounting, and payment-system boundaries.

Consolidation boundaries

  • Confirm Ingram Micro workflow and Intermedia support.
  • Compare Knowledge Hub and credential security to Click's IT Glue requirements.
  • Do not assume IT Glue or Odoo can be eliminated until contracts and parity are proven.

Decision gates

No assumptions disguised as savings. Five gates to a confident yes.

Workflow fit

Tax, quote-to-ticket-to-billing, NinjaOne matching, payments, storefront/service needs, and compliance-heavy projects pass with Click examples.

Integration fit

Ingram Micro, Intermedia, NinjaOne, TaxCloud/accounting, payment, knowledge, and credential requirements are documented with owners and exceptions.

Commercial fit

Written user pricing, onboarding fees, one-time costs, AI usage, migration, overages, and month-to-month terms match what was discussed.

Migration fit

Source data, history, API access, validation, parallel run, cutover, and internal effort are agreed before launch.

Economic fit

Verified avoidable Odoo/IT Glue spend plus realized workflow value exceeds all incremental subscription, setup, migration, and overlap costs.

The winning outcome: Click spends less time fighting the back office and more time delivering the fast, transparent service its customers already expect.
