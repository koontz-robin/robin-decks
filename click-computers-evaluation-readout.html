1. Business context

A hometown IT team ready for a simpler, more connected operating model.

Local, trusted service

Click Computers has served Central Texas since 2004 with computer repair, technology consulting, cybersecurity, backups, and managed IT support—backed by a 4.9-star Google rating from 204 reviews shown on its website.

Mixed customer model

The business combines recurring MSP services with a residential storefront that represents roughly 30% of the operation, creating both scheduled and transactional workflows.

Recent operating change

Following its transition to new ownership, Click moved the MSP onto a second Odoo instance alongside PoE Texas. The team is now evaluating whether a purpose-built platform can simplify its next stage of growth.

Click's customer promise is “fast, easy, and local.” The operating experience for the team should feel the same way.

2. Why change now?

Routine work is becoming backend configuration, manual matching, and rework.

Quoting is fragile

Click configures 25–50 quotes per week, typically spending about 15 minutes and sometimes 30 minutes per quote. New products also require backend setup and tax coding, so staff often reuse a generic “MSP hardware” item and manually edit the details.

Tax exceptions consume the day

A ten-minute task can become an hour when tax setup fails. The team described recurring friction that can surface as often as every other day.

Alerts need human translation

The custom NinjaOne integration is one-way. Alerts arrive as raw text and require manual customer matching. Click reports roughly six hours per week assigning tickets; the validation session will isolate how much of that time relates to NinjaOne alerts.

Billing remains hands-on

Staff review monthly billing, check payments daily, handle failed tokens and checks, and follow up on collections—while ticket notes and billable work remain difficult to connect.

5. Joint validation priorities

Use Click's real products, alerts, customers, and payment exceptions.

Quote, product, and tax

  • Create a new product on the fly and apply the correct tax treatment by product type and address.
  • Use price books, bundles, macros, options, proposal templates, and e-signature.
  • Validate representative Click product and tax scenarios.

NinjaOne and service

  • Validate customer and asset sync, alert association, routing, and exception handling.
  • Show labor, parts, entitlements, notes, and customer history on the ticket.
  • Measure actual match rate and time saved.

Billing and payments

  • Trace one-time and recurring quote items into service and billing.
  • Validate first-of-month runs, proration, autopay, failed tokens, checks, and collection follow-up.
  • Define TaxCloud, accounting, and payment-system boundaries.

Consolidation boundaries

  • Confirm Ingram Micro workflow and Intermedia support.
  • Compare Knowledge Hub and credential security to Click's IT Glue requirements.
  • Confirm the right long-term role for IT Glue and Odoo based on requirements and commercial terms.

6. Mutual action plan

Build confidence through real workflows, transparent economics, and a shared implementation plan.

Step 1

Review the recording, recap, and written proposal together, including user mix, onboarding, AI usage, migration assumptions, and commercial terms.

Step 2

Run a proof session using one real monitor quote, a tax edge case, representative NinjaOne alerts, a failed payment, and a check-paying customer.

Step 3

Click validates the reported six weekly ticket-assignment hours and one weekly tax-setup hour for 2–4 weeks; the team also measures quote volume, average configuration time, and overlap between workflows.

Step 4

Confirm the avoidable portion of the reported ~$1,000 monthly Odoo cost, finalize the value model with verified inputs, then agree on migration scope, cutover, and 60–90-day onboarding success criteria.

Recommendation: bring operational and executive stakeholders together for the proof so workflow fit, economics, and implementation expectations can be confirmed in one conversation.
