02 · Problems to solve

The cost is not the ticketing tool. It is work trapped between systems.

Alert intake at industrial scale

~50K–80K SolarWinds alerts/month were reported, with an earlier estimate of ~50K alerts yielding ~3K tickets. Staff manually identify sites, check for duplicates and separate primary from backup-circuit events.

Outage communication drag

Teams open and update site tickets individually. Large-event closeout messaging can take ~1.5 hours; proactive hourly updates are not feasible at current staffing.

Routing and SLA ambiguity

Tickets can cross Tier 1 shifts before escalation. Product- and customer-level SLAs are still being defined, limiting prioritization and credible compliance reporting.

Fragmented systems of record

Salesforce must remain CRM truth while billing, ticketing, SolarWinds, the customer portal and carrier workflows exchange events. Co-managed tickets increasingly run through painful email handoffs.

Economics without visibility

Support profitability by customer/product, status history, dispatch frequency and site availability are hard to obtain. Reporting depends on administrative resources and Power BI work.

Billing migration edge cases

Grandparent hierarchies, child services, auto-renew ETFs and plan/region-specific wireless pooling need proof. Wireless plus usage was described as $500K+ of revenue exposure, with period/scope not established.

Claims sourced to 13, 19 and 20 Oct. meetings. Alert, workload and revenue-risk figures are prospect estimates and require validation.

06 · Highest-value qualifying questions

Ask these before pricing or timeline becomes the decision.

What is the human work unit?
Of 50K–80K monthly alerts, how many require a person, how many become tickets, and what is measured handling time by path?
Unlocks the largest ROI claim.
What becomes cash?
Would automation avoid hires, overtime or contractors—or only redeploy capacity? Who owns the redeployment target?
Separates economic benefit from productivity.
What is the complete transition cost?
Exact IDI and Autotask terms, exit rights, credit structure, Rev.io fees, implementation, integrations and dual run?
Produces payback and three-year NPV.
Which requirements are true knockouts?
Who signs technical, operational, security and financial acceptance—and what test proves each one?
Prevents a vision demo from becoming assumed fit.
What must Salesforce own?
For every ticket/Case event, field, attachment and comment: source, direction, latency, replay, retention and authorization?
Defines architecture and effort.
Can day-one SolarWinds work be isolated?
What minimum correlation, recovery and flood-control scope must be live; what can safely phase later?
Protects schedule without automating a bad process.
What revenue is exposed by billing edge cases?
Annual revenue and invoice count by hierarchy, child-service and wireless-pooling scenario; acceptable reconciliation tolerance?
Turns “$500K+ at risk” into testable scope.
Which date is authoritative?
Direction in October, ticketing in 2026, end-of-year migration or 31 March 2027—and what must be true at each gate?
Creates a feasible critical path.
Questions prioritize missing variables that materially change ROI, technical fit or decision risk.
07 · Execute plan

Move from compelling vision to an investable decision.

1

Baseline

Run a two-week workload sample; collect contracts, costs, incident history, support reporting hours and growth hiring plan.

2

Prove

Test SolarWinds, Salesforce and outage flows with production-like data. Migrate one complex billing control group and reconcile.

3

Price

Document subscription, integrations, migration, credit and dual-run timing. Build cash, capacity, payback and downside cases.

4

Gate

Operational evaluators accept workflows; technical/security owners approve architecture; Reata reviews economics and phased plan.

  • COEO / NetWolves: Alex sponsors; Kent, Dustin, Jack, Ricky, Eric and Terry validate operations/billing; Pete confirms contracts.
  • Rev.io: Joe coordinates deep demo/integration lead/reference; product and DE teams close open proof points.
  • Decision authority: Reata Capital financial approval; individual signer and complete process still need confirmation.

Decision gates

  • Phase-one knockout requirements pass agreed tests
  • Net annual benefit and payback use validated inputs
  • Migration preserves billing accuracy and high-risk revenue
  • Engineering-approved scope supports an achievable date
  • Comparable live customer references complete diligence
Plan synthesizes agreed actions and open risks from 19–20 Oct.; recommended gates are not yet buyer commitments.
Executive decision
Proceed to a quantified proof phase—not a full-platform commitment—until COEO validates the workload baseline and Rev.io proves SolarWinds, Salesforce and complex billing workflows against agreed acceptance tests.

The upside is meaningful: a repeatable acquisition platform, redeployed service capacity and fewer systems. The current evidence supports the thesis, not yet the ROI.
