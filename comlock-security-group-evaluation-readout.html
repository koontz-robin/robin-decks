Comlock Security Group × Rev.ioCustomer evaluation summary
01 · Why change now

Evolution has become an operational liability—not the connected platform Comlock expected.

The current platform failed

Schaefer Evolution has been a “fiasco”: released too early, difficult to train, operationally unreliable, and backed by an unresponsive vendor.

Recurring billing is fragile

Roughly 200 quarterly contracts require manual billing-period entry. Each cycle consumes 4–6 hours and has recently produced duplicate tickets and double charges.

Core workflows remain offline

Inventory sits in Excel, proposals are built in Excel and Word, e-signatures require separate tools, and tax, reporting, payments, and department visibility remain fragmented.

Evaluation thesis: Rev.io must prove it can handle Comlock’s California tax logic, two-company accounting, recurring billing, truck inventory, and security workflows while remaining intuitive enough for daily adoption.
02 · Current operating model

A 65-year security business operating through disconnected systems and workarounds.

Current stack

Schaefer Evolution · two QuickBooks Online books · Bill.com · Clover · third-party recurring payments · Excel · Word · DocuSign / Adobe Sign · NMC

Comlock spans locksmithing, access control/cameras, and alarms, with retail operations and two corporations: Spectrum and Comlock.

Where work breaks

  • ~200 quarterly contracts need manual billing-period entry.
  • Duplicate tickets have caused duplicate card charges.
  • California trip-charge taxation changes by job content.
  • Inventory is managed in Excel with two annual counts.
  • Quoting runs through Excel/Word and separate e-signature tools.
  • Two corporations require two distinct QuickBooks Online books.
03 · Target future state

One controlled flow across sales, field service, inventory, recurring billing, payments, and accounting.

Modern quoting

Create polished proposals with job cost and margin, embedded e-signature, status tracking, and automatic ticket creation after acceptance.

Recurring billing control

Automate quarterly billing periods, recurring schedules, payment workflows, and customer communications without duplicates or double charges.

Field + dispatch execution

Give technicians mobile tickets, GPS/geofencing, assets, inventory, and clear job context while dispatch retains control.

Inventory integrity

Use barcode check-in/out, truck-level inventory, current ADI pricing, purchase orders, and consistent product costs.

Two-company accounting

Route invoices and payments to the correct QuickBooks Online company with separate logos, bill profiles, and books.

Usable reporting + automation

Use Revy, tax reports, technician production, department dashboards, collections workflows, and automated customer follow-up from shared data.
