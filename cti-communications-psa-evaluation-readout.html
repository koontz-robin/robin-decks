2. Why make the change now?

ConnectWise solved the old TigerPaw cloud concern, but it created a new fragmentation problem.

Renewal creates a decision clock

CTI’s ConnectWise renewal is in December, giving Bob, Dave, Tammy, and Marty a concrete window to decide whether to keep absorbing the current operational tax or move to a more unified platform.

ConnectWise onboarding left scar tissue

The previous ConnectWise rollout was described as a disaster, with migration support and go-live readiness falling short. Rev.io must prove migration, configuration, training, and day-one readiness early.

Modular tools are wearing on the team

Manage and Sell live separately, billing/payment workflows involve OneBill, OnePay, and WisePay concerns, and CTI pays for multiple bolt-ons rather than operating from one connected system.

The July demo reopened interest

Marty reacted positively to the unified Rev.io experience, the mobile app, product search, quoting, agreements, reporting, and Revii — but final decision makers still need to validate the fit themselves.

CTI Communications
3. Uncovered problems

The buying criteria are specific: sales completeness, billing confidence, and implementation trust.

Sales workflow is make-or-break

Bob and Dave “live and die” by sales functionality. Leads, opportunities, funnel visibility, quote creation, product search, proposal templates, approvals, e-signature, and quote status tracking need to be shown clearly.

Billing consolidation could block the deal

Marty flagged OneBill, OnePay, WisePay, telecom billing, and payment readiness as major validation areas. Tammy needs to see what Rev.io can consolidate day one versus what stays transitional.

Mobile experience is poor today

CTI has 9 technicians, but ConnectWise mobile is not trusted. Marty said Rev.io’s mobile app already looks better and that ConnectWise mobile “doesn’t work.”

Inventory and fulfillment are clunky

CTI needs a cleaner path from accepted quote to service ticket, inventory reservation, purchasing, deployment, serial tracking, recurring service, and billing reconciliation.

AI/account mining opportunity

CTI wants practical AI for customer-base mining, plain-language search, reporting, product/customer lists, technician note quality, and better sales follow-up — not AI theater with a cape and no utility belt.

Sales-to-operations visibility gaps

Sales does not live in Manage, operations does not live in Sell, and support questions require people to poke around separate tools. The handoff from quote to delivery to billing needs to feel native.

4. Business impact if Rev.io solves it

The value case is consolidation, faster handoffs, better technician adoption, and lower renewal risk.

23ConnectWise users in current scope; every disconnected workflow scales across the full operating team.

Reduce ConnectWise and bolt-on spend

Rev.io was positioned as typically producing 40–60% savings for companies moving from ConnectWise because the platform is not modular. CTI’s actual savings should be firmed up with current Manage, Sell, WisePay, OneBill/OnePay, and consulting costs.

Lower implementation anxiety

A clear ConnectWise API data pull, migration timeline, configuration call process, training plan, and go-live plan before December directly addresses CTI’s prior onboarding trauma.

Impact estimates are directional assumptions from the evaluation page. Exact ROI should be firmed up with current tool costs, renewal terms, ticket volume, quote volume, billing prep hours, and technician workflow restrictions.
