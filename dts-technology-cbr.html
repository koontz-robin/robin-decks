Partnership details

Start with DTS’s business goals.

DTS helps customers buy, support, and manage technology services across devices, connectivity, communications, managed router services, and IT support. This review is designed to connect Rev.io’s role to those customer-facing outcomes.

Business context

DTS combines technology sales and human support, which means the operating model depends on accurate billing, easy payments, clean service changes, and fast customer response.

Primary objectives

  • Make billing and service changes easier to manage.
  • Improve payment experience and collection visibility.
  • Support growth across connectivity, devices, VoiceLinQ, and service offerings.

Rev.io role

Rev.io supports DTS’s revenue operations foundation: billing, payment workflows, accounting alignment, and visibility into customer activity.

Where everything comes together

Connect Rev.io workflows to DTS outcomes.

This section mirrors the current CBR deck style: concise capability framing, then a discussion of where the platform can reduce work or improve customer experience.

Accounts receivable

  • Offer convenient payment options for customers.
  • Support recurring and online payment workflows.
  • Improve visibility into outstanding balances and follow-up needs.
  • Reduce avoidable payment friction for both DTS and its customers.

Billing operations

  • Keep recurring services, one-time charges, and add-ons organized.
  • Review handoffs between sales, support, provisioning, and finance.
  • Identify invoice questions or manual adjustments that can be reduced.
  • Confirm reporting needed by DTS leadership.
CBR talk track: the goal is to understand what creates drag in DTS’s revenue operation, then decide which Rev.io workflows should absorb or simplify that work next.
