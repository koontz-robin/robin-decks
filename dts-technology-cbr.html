5 · Business impact lens

Frame Rev.io value around fewer manual billing touches, cleaner cash collection, and scalable product expansion.

Manual work reduction

Assumption to validate: if DTS removes 3–5 manual billing/reconciliation hours per month, that is 36–60 hours annually returned to finance/support.

Revenue protection

For a mixed catalog of devices, plans, UC seats, router services, installs, and support, clean recurring + one-time charge capture reduces leakage risk.

Cash acceleration

Payments AR and autopay adoption can reduce collection friction; review failed-payment trends and days-to-collect to quantify the upside.

Data needed to tighten ROI: invoice volume, payment method mix, AR aging, manual adjustment count, active BoB customers, and monthly support/order volume.
