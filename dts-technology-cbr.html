Partnership details

DTS supports customers across a broad technology services portfolio.

DTS Technology helps customers buy, support, and manage services across devices, connectivity, communications, managed router services, and IT support. Rev.io’s role is to support the revenue operations behind that customer experience.

Business context

As DTS expands service offerings, the billing and payment experience should remain clear, reliable, and easy for customers to manage.

CBR objectives

  • Confirm what is working well today.
  • Identify friction in billing, payments, reporting, or customer self-service.
  • Prioritize the next Rev.io workflows that can support DTS goals.

Success criteria

A good review should leave both teams aligned on priorities, product fit, platform usage, and a clear follow-up plan.
