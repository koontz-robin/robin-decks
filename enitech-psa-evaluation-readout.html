3. Uncovered problems

The pain is concentrated where service work turns into invoice accuracy, profitability, and technician accountability.

Manual license count reconciliation

RMM, Datto EDR, RocketCyber, and other vendor seat counts fluctuate monthly. Today those counts are audited manually, which is a major finance burden and the highest-risk gap Rev.io must solve or roadmap convincingly.

Billing lives outside the PSA

Enitech invoices through QuickBooks Online today. They do not expect to migrate invoice history from the PSA; the goal is to make Rev.io the operational system of record through AR, then sync clean data into QuickBooks.

Project management and billing need to connect

Enitech runs structured cabling, AV installs, onsite work, and professional services. They need project phases, budgets, milestones, partial billing, deposits, material/labor tracking, and project profitability in one workflow.

Field tech accountability is uneven

Technicians not entering time or notes on time creates payroll, billing, and customer-communication friction. Enitech reacted positively to separate clock-in/clock-out tracking, mobile time capture, ticket notes, and onsite signatures.

Co-managed ticket sync is a gap

Bluebird Kids Health uses Freshservice, and Enitech needs bidirectional ticket sync between Rev.io and that client instance. This is not out-of-box today, but Rev.io’s public REST API creates a build path.

Vendor integration proof is required

Datto RMM billing reconciliation, Pax8 billing reconciliation, RocketCyber, Datto EDR, and potential Ninja adoption in 2027 all need a practical integration plan tied to Enitech’s actual vendor list.

The ROI case is fewer missed charges, cleaner billing ownership, and less finance/ops drag every month.

Revenue protection

Automated or API-supported license reconciliation reduces missed seat changes across RMM, EDR, security, and cloud vendors. Even a small monthly leakage rate becomes meaningful across managed service agreements.

Cleaner quote/service/invoice chain

Fewer project billing misses

Lower operational risk
