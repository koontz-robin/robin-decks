3. Uncovered problems

The buying case is concentrated in invoice accuracy, agreement flexibility, and adoption risk.

130+ invoice reviews

Month-end reconciliation is manual and cumbersome. Vendor and license counts fluctuate, while non-Pax8 products such as email backup and Informa/SaaS Alerts still require manual updates.

Rigid agreements

Enitech needs mixed billing cycles, proration, distinct onsite/remote hour pools, tiered overages, and the ability to exclude selected devices or services without making assets unserviceable.

Billing control gaps

They want covered or non-billed assets visible on invoices and a previous-invoice comparison during review. Both were flagged for product follow-up; discount lines are only an interim workaround.

Project + field friction

Autotask project management is weak, so Monday.com fills the gap. Its mobile experience is also a major pain for technicians; Enitech needs usable time, location, notes, signatures, and ticket history in the field.

Integration dependencies

FreshService bidirectional ticket sync is not native. K365’s bundled RMM/security model complicates selective per-device charging. Benji Pay token portability is still unconfirmed.

Migration + confidence risk

Attachments may complicate ticket migration; IT Glue holds documentation and Autotask asset data is unreliable. Antoine also wants long-term vendor confidence and does not want another PSA switch.

4. Business impact / ROI analysis

Use Enitech’s 130+ invoice baseline to quantify the case—not generic automation promises.

52–104hours/year saved if centralized review and reconciliation remove only 5–10 minutes per invoice across 130 invoices/month. Assumption; validate with Jessica.

Capacity returned

At a loaded finance rate of $40–$60/hour, that narrow review-time assumption is worth roughly $2.1K–$6.2K/year, before vendor-count auditing or correction work.

Revenue protection

Proration, asset-change alerts, Pax8 reconciliation, agreement components, and direct ticket/project charging reduce missed additions and delayed billing. Quantify with actual monthly credits and missed charges.

Tool consolidation

Native dashboards, projects, mobile workflows, quoting, AI, and automation may reduce dependence on Monday.com, MSP Bots/BrightGauge, or other add-ons. Savings were acknowledged but not yet priced.

Commercial baseline

Prior pricing was ~$100/user/month all-in: about $1,800–$2,200 MRR at 18–22 users, plus $2,500 onboarding split at signature/completion. Revalidate in the next call.

ROI inputs still needed: actual invoice-review minutes, reconciliation hours by vendor, corrections/credits, missed-charge rate, add-on tool spend, payment-processing cost, project volume, and staff loaded rates.
