Methodology & definitions

Primary accuracy = max(0, 1 − |Most Likely − final Closed Won| ÷ Most Likely). Accuracy is pending until the forecast month closes. Bias = forecast − actual; positive means over-forecast and negative means under-forecast. Slipped means the deal’s current close month moved beyond its forecast month. Missed win means a Closed Won deal finished in-month but was absent from the final weekly snapshot. Category quality is deal-level precision and MRR realization, not a claim that Worst/Most Likely/Best Case are cumulative ranges. Snapshot deal outcomes are refreshed from Salesforce by ID; final wins are queried independently by actual CloseDate.

Source: immutable Salesforce snapshots captured Wednesdays at 6 PM ET. Generated . Aggregates and ordinary opportunity fields only; no credentials are embedded.
