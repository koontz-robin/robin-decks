01 · What Forward Edge said

The change is about control, usable data, and growth—not feature volume.

“We’re a software sprawl and we’re getting too big… It’s not sustainable.”

Financial visibility takes too much work

Useful financial reporting can require 8+ hours of manual effort. Vendor aggregation and utilization reporting require workarounds or Cognition 360.

Sourced · 14 Aug

Core work crosses too many systems

Salesforce, QuoteWorks, ConnectWise, Monday.com, DeskDirector and Cognition 360 divide one operating process across separate tools.

Sourced · 14 Aug

VAR and onsite work are non-negotiable

Product-only sales orders, purchasing, receiving, rack/bin locations, serialization, project billing, mobile field work and school-specific scheduling all matter.

Sourced · Aug–Oct

The migration must be safe

Forward Edge has roughly 125 internal users, a long ConnectWise history, 200+ agreements, and workflows that cannot be lost in transition.

Sourced · 7 Oct
02 · Business consequences and urgency

Employee time
