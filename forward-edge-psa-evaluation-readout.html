01 · What Forward Edge said

The change is about control, usable data, and growth, not feature volume.

“We’re a software sprawl and we’re getting too big… It’s not sustainable.”

Reporting visibility takes too much work

Useful reports can require roughly eight hours of manual work. Vendor-name variations make purchasing history difficult to filter, while leadership needs views by customer, business segment, and solution set.

Core work crosses too many systems

Salesforce, QuoteWerks, ConnectWise, Monday.com, DeskDirector and Cognition 360 divide one operating process across separate tools.

“If we do a quote, it starts in Salesforce and then it moves to QuoteWorks. Then it moves from QuoteWorks to ConnectWise. Then we’re moving out of ConnectWise to Monday. So we’re just kind of all over the place with tooling.”Devan Morrison · August 14 discovery call

VAR and onsite work are non-negotiable

Product-only sales orders, purchasing, receiving, rack/bin locations, serialization, project billing, mobile field work and school-specific scheduling all matter.

The migration must be safe

Forward Edge has roughly 125 internal users, a long ConnectWise history, ~200 agreements, and workflows that cannot be lost in transition.

02 · Forward Edge strategic initiatives

The platform decision should advance efficiency, customer experience, acquisition readiness, and AI adoption.

Resolve work correctly the first time

The primary operating goal is greater efficiency: solve issues on the first interaction, eliminate callbacks, and avoid unnecessary escalations to higher-level staff.

Platform implication: technicians need trusted customer history, clear workflows, usable knowledge, and complete operational context at the point of service.

Raise the customer-experience standard

Forward Edge wants faster response and a better service experience, even though customers are not currently complaining. The objective is proactive improvement, not reactive repair.

“You can look at ConnectWise and it looks like it was built 20 years ago. It functions like it’s built 20 years ago.”Chip Logan · November 13 call

Platform implication: choose a platform that keeps innovating so Forward Edge does not repeat this evaluation in three to five years.

Build an acquisition-ready operating model

Forward Edge is in a growth phase centered on acquisitions and needs tools to analyze an acquired company’s systems, consolidate data, and standardize operations faster.

Platform implication: repeatable migration, system analysis, data normalization, and multi-entity visibility become core growth capabilities.

Scale AI capability across the workforce

AI is a major focus. Forward Edge aims to have 60% of employees Gemini AI Professional certified by year end, eventually reaching everyone, with training across finance, sales, and other teams.

“As a side item, I’ll say ConnectWise Asio is still not done. So that started, what, five years ago?”Dustin Bingham · August 14 discovery call

Rev.io shared that AI has made data migration and system consolidation substantially faster and easier.

Platform implication: evaluate both present AI capability and the vendor’s ability to deliver future innovation without compromising data trust.

03 · Business consequences and urgency

The status quo consumes capacity, delays insight, and leaves money tied up in manual operations.

Employee time

Eight-plus hours can be spent assembling a useful financial view. A purchasing agent also spends 1–5 hours each week manually updating PO tracking.

Cash flow and revenue timing

Projects, reservations and billing are separated today. Forward Edge needs clearer visibility into uninvoiced reserved items and flexible progress billing.

Capacity and rework

Scheduling in Monday.com is largely manual; resource conflicts recur; employees navigate duplicate systems and handoffs.

“So Hayden is working on two projects at one time and the actual scheduling of him on both of those projects is, without overlapping each other and like the utilization is the manual part.”Devan Morrison · September 25 initial demo

“It can literally take two or three days just to get to trying to solve the problems or trying to get information.”Chris Infante · IT Nation

Operating cost

Invoice-format changes were cited at about $2,500 and 3+ months; paid professional services were cited at about $250/hour. The confirmed core stack—ConnectWise, Monday.com, QuoteWerks, and DeskDirector—costs approximately $164,000–$165,000 annually.

Customer and employee experience

Forward Edge bought DeskDirector because the ConnectWise experience was not acceptable and currently lacks a mobile app for technicians.

Decision runway

A Q4 2026 choice preserves implementation runway before the July 2027 contract end. Rev.io estimated 4–5 months for setup, migration, testing and training.

04 · Visibility and performance gaps

Critical management views require manual work, and some service data is not trusted enough to share.

Reporting visibility

Useful reports can require roughly 8 hours per month of manual work. Purchasing history is difficult to filter across vendor-name variations, while leadership wants visibility by customer, business segment, and solution set.

“If you have to use report writer because you want to build a custom report, good luck. Absolutely good luck. That is the worst interface I’ve ever seen.”Chip Logan · August 14 discovery call

“I want to see it from a business segment, business silo aspect. I want to see it from a customer aspect. I want to see it from a solution set aspect. And I want that to be easy.”Chip Logan · August 14 discovery call

Potential impact: manual reporting and a separate customer-reporting layer add cost, administration, and decision delay.

Agreement profitability

Profitability is hard to see consistently across roughly 200 agreements. ConnectWise reporting can depend on how an agreement was invoiced, forcing third-party tools and manual exports to identify low-margin contracts. The model assumes 30 minutes/week of review effort, 75% eliminated, plus $50/month gross-profit recovery across 10 addressable agreements.

Must-have: consistent profitability visibility.

Potential impact: margin leakage and slower correction of underperforming contracts.

Lack of trust in service metrics

Service reporting relies on Cognition, custom reports, and API work, with data pulled nightly rather than in real time. The team wants faster visibility into technician performance and ticket resolution.

“We can get the data kind of, but we just don’t trust it enough to put it out there.”Chris Infante · IT Nation meeting

Potential impact: weaker coaching and accountability, less confidence in EOS scorecards, and slower identification of service issues.

05 · Workflow and experience gaps

Calendar constraints, fragmented interfaces, and email-based field work create measurable drag.

Project management calendars

The current platform assumes five eight-hour days, while field crews work four 10-hour days. Projected end dates and calendars can therefore be inaccurate.

Value: approximately 146 hours/year recovered, or $6,570/year at $45/hour.

Assumes 10 technicians × 15 minutes/week plus one scheduling hour/week, with 80% eliminated. Schedule corrections only; mobile savings are separate.

Field technicians lack a usable mobile app

Technicians are not using the ConnectWise mobile experience and rely heavily on email.

“The mobile app on the phone with ConnectWise is unusable … It’s terrible.”Chris Infante · IT Nation meeting

Value: 40 hours/month or 480 hours/year redirected toward service work; approximately $21,600/year.

10 techs × 3 visits/day × 20 days/month × 5 minutes × 80% adoption. Dispatcher savings excluded.

Outdated UI/UX creates another interface

The portal and technician experience were described as antiquated, slow to refresh, and burdened with too many fields to train users on. Forward Edge added DeskDirector as a separate customer-facing layer.

“Their service desk is absolute garbage.”Chris Infante · IT Nation meeting

Value: approximately $5,000–$6,000/year in addressable software spend if customer access and experience are preserved.

Potential impact: slower staff and customer workflows, added administration, and avoidable software cost.

Required fields by ticket type

Not available today7 items

Quote creation from a ticket

Current status: Rev.io does not currently provide a smooth, direct path to initiate a quote from a ticket. Quote-to-ticket visibility is one-way: a quote can show tickets created from it, but the ticket does not display the related quote. Forward Edge’s priority is an easy ticket-to-quote workflow, ideally with the resulting quote visible on the ticket.

Project Gantt filtering by assignee

Current status: Assignments are supported in the table view, but the Gantt view cannot currently be filtered by individual assignee.

Multi-assignee profitability allocation

Current status: Profitability is currently divided equally across multiple assignees. Proportional allocation by hours may be possible from existing data but is not implemented.

Salesforce task/activity synchronization

Distributor ETA and shipment tracking

What does a customer see in the Rev.io customer portal?

Customers can create and view tickets, exchange public notes and attachments, review invoices, make payments, update autopay, export invoices, and, when permitted, add contacts who can access the portal. Announcements can also appear on the home page.

Product roadmap

Is Rev.io's roadmap published?

Rev.io has an interactive internal roadmap application with granular feature-level tracking that it can review with customers.

API integrations

Can Forward Edge use Rev.io's API data to build custom functionality as it currently does with ConnectWise?

Teacher portal onboarding

Would Forward Edge have to import all teachers and create portal users before a new teacher could submit a ticket?

Portal access

Can a portal link be placed on teachers' desktops or on a website?

Yes, but Rev.io is not an RMM and would not deploy the link itself; another tool or person would need to place it.

Ticket intake

How does Rev.io handle ticket emails when the sender's exact name is not recognized?

Rev.io uses intelligent domain matching. If both domain and name matching fail, unmatched tickets are placed in a list so staff can route them to the correct customer.

That would lose the identity and location information needed to know where to route the ticket, so it was not considered a suitable solution.

Distributor integrations

Quote creation from a ticket

Service Management / Inventory Session37 Q&A
Inventory scanning

Is there a scanner for scanning large numbers of serial numbers?

Yes. HID- and wedge-enabled scanners plugged into a computer are supported for serial numbers and UPCs, and the mobile app can scan with the phone camera.

Agreement invoicing

Do multiple agreements or billing cycles lead to multiple invoices for the same customer?

They can, but the invoices can be consolidated.

Agreement component routing

If time is logged a certain way, does it route to a particular agreement component?

It depends on the reason code selected and the product tied to that reason code; the product may be configured as a covered item in that component.

Expiring-products billing

When will billing for expiring products be available in the new Rev.io platform?

It exists in Classic Rev.io and was on the roadmap to be brought into the new platform by February.

Agreement workflow implementation

Will Forward Edge need to create a project every time a new agreement needs to be signed?

No. An order can act as a smaller project with its own workflow steps, so a full project template is not required for a single service-delivery flow.

Hardware as a Service

Can the platform support a Hardware-as-a-Service model?

Yes. Rev.io supports attaching an asset to an agreement and billing an amount each time the agreement renews.

Agreement profitability

If tickets are created outside the agreement, are they included in that agreement's margin calculation?

No. They are reflected in overall customer metrics and customer-level reports, but not in the agreement's profitability because they were not driven by that agreement.

Component profitability reporting

When reason codes route time entries to different agreement components, can profitability be viewed independently for each component?

Yes. Component summaries show the applicable margin and profitability, with a further detailed breakdown available.

Van stock workflow

Can inventory be tracked per van, assigned to a ticket when pulled, decremented automatically, and trigger a minimum-stock notification for that van?

Yes. Rev.io described this as an exact supported use case: locations can represent warehouses, bins, or trucks; inventory can be consumed on tickets; and the inventory can be controlled without sending every debit, credit, gain, loss, or variance to accounting.

Reserved inventory

What does 'reserved' inventory mean?

It means the item has been set aside for a job but has not yet been billed.

Inventory purchasing

Can multiple required items or quantities across tickets be ordered together?

Yes. Multiple items can be selected, purchase orders are automatically split by default vendor, and orders can cover multiple tickets or an entire project.

QuickBooks integration

Are invoices batched when they sync to QuickBooks?

No. Each invoice syncs one-for-one and element-for-element to preserve item-level visibility and QuickBooks reporting.

Invoice detail

Can ticket time entries be shown on or omitted from an invoice?

Yes. For service-type tickets, the template can be configured to show the time, and the recommended approach is to use AI to turn ticket notes into a clean work-summary paragraph.

AI invoice summaries

Does Forward Edge need to create and populate a custom field to put a ticket summary on the invoice?

No. Rev.io can use AI to summarize the existing ticket notes into a paragraph without consuming Revise credits.

Invoice templates

Is there one invoice template for all customers, or can templates vary by customer?

The template defines the system-wide structure, headers, and charge areas, but a separate template can also be built for an individual customer.

Suppressing invoice charges

Can project-management fees or similar components be hidden on invoices for customers who should not see them?

Rev.io proposed using a package and configuring whether the package's component charges are suppressed on the invoice; the presenter expected that to meet the need.

Payments

What payment methods and billing controls does native Rev.io Payments support?

It supports ACH and credit cards, customer-specific surcharging and late fees, autopay, direct payment processing in Rev.io, and customer self-service payment through the portal, with native reporting and chargeback visibility.

Collections automation

Are collection plans email-based, such as messages at 31, 61, and 91 days?

Yes. Plans can automate invoice resends and emails or letters at configured intervals, then add the account to a dashboard call list after a threshold; small balances can be suppressed.

Collections notes

Is there a notes area to record why payment is delayed?

Yes. Notes are stored at the account level. Opening the account from the collections list shows the notes, including why payment is delayed and who last spoke with the customer.

Collections history and pausing

Does the account show collections emails already sent, and what happens when a collections sequence is delayed?

Sent collections emails are shown. Collections can be suspended, but the current behavior restarts the configured sequence from the beginning when it resumes; Rev.io said enhancements were coming to streamline this.

Nonpayment status flags

Can an account be visibly flagged when nonpayment has a known reason, such as pending grant funding, incomplete work, a purchase order, or board approval?

Yes. Custom tags or a hot note can flag the account. Tags can be filtered and reported on, and the account can be removed from or suspended in a collections template when needed.

Promise to Pay

Is the Classic Rev.io 'Promise to Pay' feature available in the new platform?

No. It does not exist in the new platform, and Rev.io was unsure whether it would be brought over; if it were, it likely would not work the same way because the Classic implementation pays off the balance and is accounting-wise unusual.

Project dispatching

Can project work be scheduled through the dispatch board or directly from a project-oriented view?

A projects board can be created so project tickets and team schedules appear together in day or week views. Rev.io agreed direct scheduling from the project screen would be useful but described the projects board as the current approach.

Department capacity planning

Would a project board work for a department such as marketing to see overall team availability?

Yes. Rev.io recommended making each department its own representative group so that group's members and availability can be selected and viewed together.

Field location tracking

How does the dispatch map determine technician location and whether the technician is traveling or on a job?

While a technician tracks travel or job time in the mobile app, the map shows the technician's location and uses icon color to distinguish states. Tracking is tied to the customer address, and the app automatically geofences arrival.

Calendar integration

Does the dispatch board synchronize with Outlook and Google Calendar?

Yes. It uses two-way synchronization and combines calendar events with each employee's working hours and company holidays to show availability.

Mobile app demonstration

Can the mobile app be demonstrated and streamed to the conference display?

Yes. The presenter planned to stream it from a phone to the conference display; the demo was deferred to dinner.

Ticket consolidation and revenue mapping

When multiple service teams work the same project, can their time be logged on one ticket and still map billable revenue correctly?

Yes. Time can be separated by reason code on one ticket, billing can differ from how work is logged, and GL accounts can be mapped by product with multiple products on the same ticket. Separate tickets can also still be combined into one bill.

School district ticket organization

How are tickets organized when multiple schools and multiple buildings submit their own tickets?

Each school district or school is treated as a distinct customer, with buildings represented as locations under that customer. Saved views can show a technician's assigned building or district, or provide a broader view across customers and buildings.

Ticket work flows against the agreement through its service tickets. Non-ticket time such as administrative work can be entered directly against the customer's agreement, as in ConnectWise.

Rev.io understood it as a presentation layer that reskins the ConnectWise customer portal. The ticket and fields remain in ConnectWise; Desk Director displays them but does not own the underlying workflow or data.

The current projects list shows all projects and their dates. A master Gantt/calendar view previously existed but was removed because it became unwieldy; Rev.io is redesigning it and plans to restore it as its own tab.

Weekly project review

Yes. The projects list can be filtered by owner and customer and shows open projects, next milestones, and recent notes.

Project statuses

Internal projects

A project does not need to be directly managed through a customer ticket. Forward Edge can use its own internal account in Rev.io for internal projects and add tasks or tickets as needed.

Tagging and notifications

Does Rev.io support Monday.com-style tagging, comments, and automatic notifications when work is assigned or changes stage?

Rev.io can assign a task to someone and notify them, send custom notifications, and use workflows to notify people on status changes. Direct @-mentioning within a line item is not available yet, although Rev.io wants to add it. Tickets can also be tagged and can carry additional associated text.

Rev.io believes the core workflow can be replicated, though it would look different. It would likely use an internal ticket within a project, custom fields, multiple participants, workflow notifications, and a template that prepopulates requirements. The teams agreed to review the detailed process with Forward Edge's director in a follow-up session.

Project time logs

Yes.

Completed-contract billing

It can. Because revenue is generally deferred until the final invoice, customers may need to reconcile and recognize revenue manually during the project based on other metrics, especially for long-running projects.

Progress billing

Can Revii show gross margin globally and for a specific ticket or project?

Yes, but the user must ask Revii for the desired scope rather than having it persistently available in the project interface.

Revii capacity and pricing

If all 150 Forward Edge users use Revii heavily, could token usage cause availability problems or throttling?

Rev.io said availability would not be the issue. Accounts receive a monthly credit allotment and pay for overage usage. Basic chat does not consume credits, while actions do. Per-user usage reporting is available, and alerts and user-level throttling are being developed to prevent one user from consuming the company's allotment.

Revii usage and pricing

How common are credit overages among Rev.io customers, and what do they cost?

Rev.io said customers use some level of overage, but the average overage is not high. The stated price was approximately one cent per token.

Revii report credit usage

Does a Revii-built report consume credits every time it runs, or only when it is built?

Building the report consumes credits. Once the report is published, users can run the same report repeatedly without additional credit consumption.

Revii credit usage

Do customer summaries, general chat, help instructions, and other in-application summaries consume Revii credits?

No. General chat, how-to guidance, and in-application summaries do not consume credits. Credits are primarily consumed by outcome-oriented actions. If Revii fails to produce an outcome, the action does not count.

Report building

How technical must a user be to create a report or dashboard with Revii?

Very little technical skill is required. Entry-level users who have never built reports can use natural language, and Revii asks follow-up questions when the request lacks enough detail.

Project forecast reporting

Is there a report for comparing delivered project value against a monthly or quarterly project quota?

Rev.io would create a dashboard for this. The required invoice and project data is available, but the dashboard would need to be configured rather than relying on the demonstrated built-in view.

Percentage of completion

Can a dashboard show percentage of completion against budgeted project or ticket hours and the remaining gap to a quarterly goal?

Yes. Budgeted time is a native project value at multiple levels and accumulates against actual tracked time. A dashboard can visualize progress with options such as pie charts or speedometers.

Recurring revenue reporting

Can a service lead's or sales representative's quota dashboard include recurring agreement revenue expected in a quarter?

Yes. Recurring revenue is available as data and can be included or excluded when the dashboard is built. Rev.io noted that some customers include only net-new revenue after the first month, so onboarding would help configure the preferred treatment.

Revii credit reset

When does Revii token or credit usage reset?

Every month.
