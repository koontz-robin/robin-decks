Executive summary

The decision is whether one operating record can serve three distinct motions.

Grey Matter’s current Halo environment does not cleanly support both recurring MSP work and project-led security/AV delivery. The result is parallel quoting and project tools, spreadsheets, manual re-entry, weak field capture, and billing risk. Rev.io should advance only after Grey Matter’s operators prove the workflows, accounting boundary, integrations, migration, and unresolved gaps with representative data.

Company context

A nationwide technology partner with recurring and project-based delivery.

Grey Matter publicly positions itself as a premium technology partner founded in 1996, serving customers nationwide with offices in Connecticut, New York, and Texas. Its published services span managed IT, cloud, support and consulting, network and cyber security, monitoring, surveillance, VoIP, backup, audio visual, pre-wire/electrical, and integrated-building solutions.

Three operating divisions

Discovery describes core MSP/help desk, physical security, and audio-visual project work. A separate VoIP sister company with its own EIN was stated to be out of scope.

Current systems

Halo PSA, QuickBooks Online, Projects 360, PandaDoc, HubSpot, CloudRadial, N-central, Hudu, Rapid Response, and distributor/vendor workflows are part of the stated environment or evaluation boundary.

User profile

The record cites roughly 30 Halo licenses, 5–10 field technicians working onsite daily, and a majority desk-based user population. Final role and seat mapping is still needed.

Why change now

Fragmentation is creating work, uncertainty, and avoidable revenue risk.

The discovery record supports the problem pattern, but most annual economics remain unquantified. Consequences below are framed as risks or reported examples—not guaranteed savings.

Quote duplication

Security/AV proposals may be built in Projects 360 or PandaDoc and recreated in Halo, creating manual effort and tax/line-item mismatch risk.

Project margin is external

Project P&L is maintained in spreadsheets; financial visibility is limited to the people who manage those files.

Progress billing is manual

Milestone structures such as 20/50/30, retainage, and billing triggers require external tracking and can be late or missed.

Field data arrives late

Mobile workflows are weak and technicians may batch notes and time at week-end, risking missed detail, inaccurate time, and unbilled overtime or project labor.

Recurring revenue can leak

License quantity changes and non-integrated vendor services are not consistently reflected in recurring billing; Barracuda spot checks reportedly found material missed revenue, but no validated annual baseline was supplied.

Procurement lacks a shared view

Teams struggle to see what was quoted, ordered, received, and still missing. New or rebranded security parts can require extensive manual catalog entry.

Desired outcomes

Build a usable, reconciled operating record.

Translate interest into measurable acceptance criteria before commercial commitment.

One quote-to-cash path

Reduce re-keying while preserving customer-ready proposals, option handling, hidden quantities, taxes, approvals, change history, and project handoff.

Trusted project economics

Give authorized users visibility into budget, committed and actual parts/labor, change orders, invoices, margin, and remaining work.

Reliable recurring billing

Compare vendor/RMM/license quantities to billed services, resolve exceptions, and activate recurring items at controlled milestones.

Field-ready execution

Capture assignments, time, notes, photos, parts, and status in connected and no-connectivity conditions without week-end reconstruction.

Reconciled accounting

Prove how customers, items, invoices, credits, payments, POs, tax, deposits, and corrections move to QuickBooks Online and close.

Phased adoption

Sequence security/AV and MSP scope based on proven fit, with training, parallel controls, explicit exit gates, and rollback.

Requirements & boundaries

Demo evidence is a starting point—not acceptance.

Capabilities and integrations mentioned in meetings must be validated against Grey Matter’s tenant, data, contracts, and edge cases. Roadmap statements are not commitments.
Quote presentation and conversion
Critical

Prove hidden quantities/unit economics, optional systems, multi-address tax, versioning, signatures, catalog search, quote duplication, and conversion into the correct project/order structure. System-type-filtered customer output was not available in the demo.

Project financial control
Critical

Reconcile budget, actual labor/material, committed cost, specific/average inventory cost, margin, remaining work, and equipment-versus-consumable reporting.

Progress billing, retainage & AIA
Known gap

Test deposit, fixed, percentage, milestone, and final invoicing. Native AIA/retainage support was not established; finance must approve any export or controlled workaround.

Change orders and expenses
Open

Prove additive and deductive changes, signatures, linkage to project budget/billing, and field expenses. Do not rely on discussed future order or expense features.

PO, receiving & inventory
Critical

Trace quoted need → vendor choice → PO → partial receipt → serialized/nonserialized inventory → project cost → invoice; validate internal receipt notifications and returns.

Mobile and offline
Critical

Field technicians should prove offline caching, assignments, time, notes, photos, signatures, parts, sync recovery, and supervisor review in a realistic low-connectivity scenario.

Recurring-service reconciliation
Critical

Test Pax8, N-central Pro, SentinelOne, Cove/Cove 365, Barracuda, and non-integrated vendor exceptions. Automatic correction was not demonstrated; define who reviews and posts changes.

QuickBooks Online
Critical

Map direction, timing, customers, items, taxes, invoices, deposits, credits, payments, POs/vendor bills, classes/entities, duplicates, errors, close, and reconciliation ownership.

Integration boundary
Validate

Confirm production scope and directionality for HubSpot, CloudRadial/API, N-central, Hudu, Rapid Response, Teams/Slack, ADI/Portal.io, Ingram, TD Synnex, ScanSource, Pax8, and other named vendors.

Client intake / co-managed portal
Known gap

Customer-specific forms with predefined fields and mature co-managed workflows were not available as described. Define minimum acceptable portal workflow without assuming roadmap delivery.

Tailored proof plan

Six scenarios. Grey Matter-owned pass/fail evidence.

For each scenario capture sample, owner, expected result, actual result, exception, workaround/control impact, and sign-off.

Security/AV quote to project

Load a real sanitized multi-system, multi-address quote; hide quantities, present options, sign, convert, order parts, and preserve scope and economics.

Project billing and close

Run a 20/50/30-style project with retainage/AIA needs, change orders, partial receipts, labor, final invoice, and margin reconciliation.

Field offline day

Have field technicians receive work, capture time/notes/photos/parts offline, reconnect, resolve conflicts, and obtain supervisor approval.

Recurring leakage check

Compare Pax8 plus one non-integrated vendor or uploaded file to recurring services; identify missing/changed quantities and prove controlled correction.

Accounting exception

Send a controlled batch to QBO, force a mapping or duplicate failure, correct it, and reconcile source, tax, AR/AP, payments, and job totals.

MSP service operation

Test intake, ticket merge, agreements/included hours, overage, asset/serial context, N-central alert flow, customer reporting, and permissions.
