2. Why make the change now?

The SkySwitch launch creates a narrow window to avoid rework.

Hyperion is already in motion: one SkySwitch customer is porting soon, another long-time managed services customer is expected to follow, and 60-100 White Label seats may migrate if SkySwitch proves better for customers.

Direct now, migrate once

Using SkySwitch's wholesale Rev.io instance first would require a full migration later if Hyperion wants to add White Label or contract directly with Rev.io.

Billing needs to be ready quickly

Hyperion asked whether September 1 was realistic. Rev.io's conservative range is 60-90 days, with a focused SkySwitch-first path potentially compressing toward roughly 45 days.

Manual controls are already strained

White Label seat changes rely on service desk communication to finance. When that handoff misses, billing changes can be missed too.

The strategic shift is not just a new switch. It is Hyperion deciding whether telecom billing becomes a controlled growth platform or another monthly reconciliation exercise.

3. Uncovered problems

The current process depends on people catching what systems should expose.

Seat changes are not reliably reaching finance

  • Customer requests like adding five extensions do not automatically sync from White Label to finance.
  • Service desk tickets must be translated into billing changes manually.
  • Hyperion said those changes get missed.

Reconciliation reporting is constrained

  • White Label does not let Hyperion run reconciliation reports independently.
  • The report must be requested after billing each month.
  • Finance is left blind until the report arrives.

Platform sequencing can create migration waste

  • Starting with SkySwitch's wholesale instance would separate the initial SkySwitch setup from the eventual direct Rev.io environment.
  • Adding White Label later would mean redefining the engagement and scripting data into a new database.

Technical fit needs proof

  • Rev.io handles carrier usage and CDR data, but not out-of-the-box MACD provisioning sync.
  • Hyperion needs to see reporting, workflow alerts, ConnectWise catalog sync, and importer behavior in a technical demo.

Rev.io's value case is revenue protection, fewer monthly controls, and avoiding duplicate migration effort.

Leakage protection

Unbillable usage reporting can flag active extensions or phone numbers that are not tied to a customer. Even a small number of missed seats matters against roughly 600 near-term seats across White Label and SkySwitch.

Faster month-end confidence

If finance can self-serve exception reporting instead of waiting on White Label after billing, month-end review shifts from report chasing to exception resolution.

Cleaner customer communication

If SkySwitch customers go live before billing is fully configured, Rev.io can support historical usage back-billing and provide customer email templates for billing changes.

Budgetable pricing

Rev.io discussed a recurring-revenue percentage model with an upfront flat dollar bucket, reviewed annually if growth significantly exceeds the agreed band.

5. Requirements and adjacent systems

The implementation should match Hyperion's operating stack, not force a billing island.

Switches and usage

  • Build SkySwitch integration first.
  • Add White Label integration 1-2 months after SkySwitch stabilizes.
  • Use a single importer strategy that can de-duplicate seats moving between platforms.

PSA and accounting

  • ConnectWise PSA remains the operating system for service workflow.
  • Rev.io product catalog should match one-to-one with ConnectWise.
  • QuickBooks Online remains accounting system of record.

Payments and inventory

  • Nexus Point remains payment processor.
  • Mobius currently syncs ConnectWise to QBO inventory.
  • Mobius may sunset by year-end if FlexPlane's ConnectWise to QBO inventory sync replaces it.

Tax and compliance

  • Business is currently Michigan only.
  • Compliance scope is FCC plus one state.
  • JSI supports compliance through Jared Harper and Matt Dean.

Billing controls

  • Unbillable usage reporting for extensions and numbers not tied to customers.
  • Finance workflow alerts when changes need billing review.
  • Back-billing capability for historical usage if timing requires it.

Commercial fit

  • All customers are on automatic ACH.
  • No collections issue was identified.
  • Cost recovery fee is already being passed through as pure margin.
