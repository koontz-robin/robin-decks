After more than seven years, ConnectWise feels cumbersome and creates recurring support issues. Infinite needs a modern platform that fits a hardware-heavy security business and a mobile workforce.

Where Rev.io can help

Connect distributor-backed quoting, purchasing, receiving, inventory, project delivery, technician mobile work, service, invoicing, and QuickBooks handoff.

Infinite Technologies
02 · What we heard

Infinite needs less complexity without losing control of hardware, jobs, or customer service.

Inventory is the major pain point

Security jobs include cameras, switches, strikes, mag locks, and other serialized or allocated equipment. Purchasing, receiving, stock, job allocation, cost, and billing must stay connected.

Primary proof gate

Licensing should match roles

Infinite currently pays for 20 standard PSA users even though 12 technicians primarily need mobile access. The future package should align permissions and price to actual work.

Quoting is manual

BOM sheets, proposal templates, Adobe e-signature, and manual opportunity tracking create handoffs and make margin and status harder to manage consistently.

Projects carry real complexity

Milestones, labor, materials, status, field execution, and billing must reflect a realistic surveillance or access-control project—not a generic service example.

Existing tools must fit

Ninja, QuickBooks, ScreenConnect, ADI, TD Synnex, ScanSource, Wesco, CDW, and Ingram Micro require explicit scope and ownership.

Migration must earn trust

Years of ConnectWise history need a scoped validation, training, cutover, reconciliation, and support plan that protects daily operations.

05 · Commercial and value boundary

Price parity makes operational value—not subscription savings—the real business case.

Current subscription baseline

ConnectWise PSA was discussed at $1,250/month for 20 users.

Rev.io baseline discussed

Rev.io was discussed at $1,240/month for the 20-person mix—only $10/month lower before other costs.

One-time investment shown

$5,000 total: $2,500 setup/training plus $2,500 migration. Confirm onboarding charges, taxes, overlap, and internal effort.

A credible ROI must validate parts and labor capture, time avoided, and software actually retired—without counting the same value twice.

No customer-validated hard ROI is claimed here. The $10/month price difference alone does not justify a platform transition.
