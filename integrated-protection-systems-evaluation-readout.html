3. Current systems + challenges / DBMs

The DBMs are rooted in system fragmentation and weak visibility across the full quote-to-cash lifecycle.

Simpro

Used for quoting, scheduling, inventory, projects, and invoicing. Challenges: clunky workflows, hard service-contract setup, limited job-cost confidence, and project/ticket handoffs that do not give IPS the operating visibility they want.

Zoho

Used for service tickets only, not as a true CRM. Challenges: limited help desk functionality, difficult reporting on response/resolution/open/oldest tickets, and email-threading friction across customer communication.

QuickBooks

Accounting system of record. Challenge/DBM: IPS needs Rev.io to handle operational billing, invoices, payments, and recurring charges cleanly while syncing the right accounting data into QuickBooks.

ConnectTeam / Verizon Connect

GPS/fleet tracking is separate from job execution. Challenge: technician location and time data are not naturally tied to tickets, dispatch, job costing, and customer/account history.

Paycom

Payroll clock-in remains separate from job time. Challenge: technicians can have three daily clock-ins, making missed entries common and weakening labor accuracy, P&L visibility, and billing confidence.

No true CRM

Sales activity is not fully tracked today, and the president doubles as sales management. DBM: IPS needs lead/opportunity visibility, quote history, customer context, and growth reporting in one place.
