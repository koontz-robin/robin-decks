Role context

Joe’s Strategic AE territory is part of Rev.io’s Strategic Account Executive model. Strategic AEs own larger, more complex accounts that require deeper discovery, stronger executive alignment, and more regional relationship-building.

Because Strategic AEs are expected to travel more and build market presence, territories are assigned geographically rather than purely by inbound ownership or account volume.

Strategic account qualification

An account qualifies for Strategic AE ownership if it meets either of the following criteria:

1
35+ employees

Accounts with 35 or more employees should route to a Strategic AE, even if communications billing is lower.

2
$200,000+ billing

Accounts with $200K+ in communications billing should route to a Strategic AE, even below 35 employees.

Simple rule: either threshold is enough. If the account hits employee count or communications billing, ownership should be reviewed for Strategic AE routing.

Communications billing note

Communications billing volume is not currently tracked proactively in Salesforce. Until that is solved, this qualification will usually surface during discovery.

If any rep discovers that an account has $200,000+ in communications billing, ownership should be reviewed and routed to the Strategic AE team.

Practical guidance for Joe

Use the territory map as the primary ownership guide and prioritize accounts in the blue states that meet Strategic AE qualification. Joe’s focus should be on larger, more complex prospects where regional presence, executive engagement, travel, and deeper account planning can create leverage.
