4 · Manager Forecast Rollup

How do manager forecasts roll up to you every week, and how do we raise the quality of war-room deals?

Leadership working session · November 2026
MONData hygieneAE updates stage, amount, close date, next step, business issue and contract timing.
TUEManager inspectionRemove weak deals, challenge slippage and confirm named forecast categories.
WEDTeam war roomOnly Stage 3+ deals that pass quality gates. Solve proof, stakeholder and timing gaps.
THUExecutive rollupManagers submit commit, most likely, upside, risk, ICP mix and changes from last week.
FRIAction closureOwners close escalations; unresolved risks roll into next week with dates.
QUALITY

War-room entry gate

ICP fit · quantified business issue · decision process · credible close date · scheduled next step.
OWNERSHIP

Manager certification

Every forecasted deal is reviewed; any override includes written evidence and a date.
CADENCE

Executive inspection

Focus on changes, gaps and asks. Leaders should not rebuild the manager forecast live.
Quality goal: fewer deals in the room, more evidence per deal, and a forecast that changes only when buyer evidence changes.
