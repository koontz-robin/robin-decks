01 · Why change now

The company has demand and loyal customers—but its operating system is two overloaded people.

Work falls through the cracks

Technician notes, follow-ups, and customer commitments depend on word of mouth. Customers sometimes call weeks later because nobody followed up.

Manual work limits capacity

Two leaders share scheduling, hiring, training, quoting, and billing. One billing owner is leaving, increasing urgency around process continuity.

Recurring revenue is the foundation

Roughly 250 fire alarm inspection and monitoring contracts create dependable revenue—but scheduling and invoicing remain fragmented and manual.

Evaluation thesis: Rev.io wins if it removes administrative load now while giving Life Safety Security a controlled path to grow beyond its current six-person low-voltage team.

02 · Current operating model

Every core workflow crosses systems—or lives on paper.

Current stack

Housecall ProQuickBooksSpreadsheetsWord + PDFDocuSign (unused by division)Offsite inventorySecuritas → CMS transition

Housecall Pro manages dispatch, QuickBooks manages billing and payments, spreadsheets track inspections, and quoting begins with calculators, paper, and vendor phone calls.

Where work breaks down

  • 10–20 invoices a week are manually rebuilt in QuickBooks.
  • Inspection scheduling consumes about eight hours every month.
  • Technicians remove inventory without logging it; jobs miss parts weekly.
  • Manual pricing previously contributed to an estimated $10K job loss.
  • Rural connectivity creates field time and note-capture risk.
  • Sales commissions are tracked on a scratch pad.
Current stateDisconnected systems · manual controls · operational risk
03 · Target future state

A connected workflow designed around recurring fire and security service.

One-year service planning

Schedule annual inspections in advance and automate recurring invoices instead of rebuilding the monthly calendar from a spreadsheet.

Catalog-driven quoting

Use live ADI pricing, vendor imports, Portal.io comparisons, and margin controls to replace paper calculations and stale Word proposals.

Quote-to-ticket automation

Move an approved, electronically signed proposal directly into service execution without rekeying customer and job information.

Field accountability

Use offline mobile workflows, geofencing, travel/work timers, notes, and GPS visibility to improve technician compliance.

Inventory control

Scan parts in and out, track locations and serialized assets, and stop discovering missing material on the day of a scheduled job.

Connected billing + CMS

Use Rev.io for invoicing and payments, reduce QuickBooks to the general ledger, and synchronize monitoring accounts through CMS.

Future stateOne database · one workflow · less administrative drag
