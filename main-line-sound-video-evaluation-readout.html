2. Business issue + why Rev.io fits

Main Line needs one operational backbone that keeps the AV sales experience polished while making service, projects, contracts, and revenue easier to manage.

The business issue

Main Line’s work spans proposals, projects, service tickets, recurring contracts, inventory, billing, payments, and customer communication. Today that workflow is fragmented across DTools, Outlook, QuickBooks, payment tools, and manual follow-up.

Why Rev.io fits Main Line

Rev.io’s strength is the operational back end Main Line wants cleaned up: ticket intake, dispatch, mobile technician work, parts-needed queues, agreements, invoicing, payments, reporting, and revenue visibility.

Why Main Line fits Rev.io

Main Line is exactly the kind of integrator Rev.io PSA was rebuilt for: project-heavy, field-service driven, recurring-service aware, and ready to connect sales, operations, and finance without forcing every team into separate systems.

3. Uncovered problems

The pain is still fragmentation — but Friday clarified exactly where Rev.io is winning and where Portal must carry the front-end proposal experience.

Service coordination is too manual today

Main Line currently switches between DTools, Outlook, separate payment tools, and manual notifications. Rev.io’s grouped-by-status tickets, custom boards, inline edits, templates, and unassigned queues directly answered this pain.

Parts-needed workflow is a DTools gap

Today, parts requests require separate emails to purchasing. Rev.io can let technicians mark “parts needed,” notify purchasing, and route work into a dedicated queue.

Quoting remains the core risk

Pete and Matt Gorman care most about quoting, visual proposal appearance, and project visibility. Product images live in Rev.io’s catalog but do not yet flow into Rev.io-generated quotes, making Portal the preferred visual proposal path.

Inventory visibility needs a clean answer

Portal does not show live Rev.io inventory, so quote builders may need to cross-reference Rev.io inventory while building proposals in Portal. That workflow needs to be made explicit before leadership review.
