2. Why make the change now?

Main Line is trying to modernize the back end without breaking the sales motion that already works.

DTools works up front, struggles after acceptance

Matt walked through DTools opportunity, room/location, catalog, labor, proposal, deposit, and payment-link workflows. The bigger pain is what happens after the proposal is accepted.

Recurring + service work is getting more complex

3. Uncovered problems

The pain is fragmentation: inventory, billing, service, contracts, and quoting all touch different parts of the operation.

Inventory split across systems

QuickBooks is the main inventory source, while DTools carries truck stock, showroom inventory, and service vehicle inventory. Cleanup is ongoing and time-consuming.

Security billing requires duplicate work

Security invoices from the acquired company are still flowing through separate QuickBooks workflows, then manually recreated, and cannot be tied cleanly to project payments.

DTools post-acceptance friction

DTools project management handles tasks, ordering, and installation status, but frequent UI changes, fragmented inventory, and contract visibility gaps create drag.

Quoting adoption risk

Sales is the hardest group to convince. Terminology differences and screen complexity in Rev.io could slow adoption unless Portal IO or a clearer quote workflow solves the gap.

4. Business impact if Rev.io solves it

The business case is a measurable productivity lift across billing, service, inventory, and contract visibility.

30%productivity improvement is the buyer’s stated threshold to justify the cost and disruption of replacing the core platform.

Eliminate duplicate billing steps

Security monitoring, access control, AV service plans, Total Connect, project/progress invoices, AutoPay, and portal payments can be managed in one billing workflow.

Improve contract profitability

Agreements can group service types, blocks of hours, line items, assets, tickets, and customer profitability so Main Line can see which contracts are underwater.

Reduce back-office service drag

Tickets can connect customer, asset, dispatch, technician status, parts used, photos/videos, ready-for-billing status, invoice, and payment without manual handoffs.

Protect sales velocity with Portal IO

Portal IO can either feed live distributor pricing into Rev.io quotes or remain the proposal/design front end while Rev.io ingests signed proposal data.
