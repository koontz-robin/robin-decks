Metric definitions

So nobody has to decode the Batcomputer during the team meeting.
Discovery Set: Discovery Call events set in-week by Date_Appt_Was_Set__c, matching the Salesforce Discovery Set report.
Discovery Complete: Discovery Call events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Discovery Complete Influenced: Completed Discovery Call events credited by Event SDR_Influence__c.
Cbrs Set: Client Business Review events set in-week by Date_Appt_Was_Set__c, matching the discovery-set methodology.
Cbrs Complete: Client Business Review events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Client Followups Complete: Client Follow Up events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Initial Expansion Complete: Initial Expansion Meeting events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Payments Quote Reviews Complete: Payments Quote Review events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Initial Demos Ran: Initial Demo events scheduled in-week and marked Completed by the end-of-week cutoff.
Follow Up Pricing Complete: Completed Follow Up Demo / Meeting, Payments Follow-up, and Pricing / Negotiation events scheduled in-week.
Sdr Sourced Opps: Opportunities created with SDR_Influence__c populated and not None.
Mqls Converted: Marketing-sourced opportunities created, excluding tradeshow, sales/outbound, partner/channel, and referral sources.
Tradeshow Leads Converted: Opportunities created with Marketing_Source__c = Tradeshow.
Booked Mrr: Closed-won Opportunity Amount by CloseDate, grouped by Product_Type__c.
