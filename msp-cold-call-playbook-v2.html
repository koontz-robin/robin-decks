MSP Cold Call Flow

Keep this visible during calls. Follow the sequence, personalize the separation, use the MSP value statement, then close for the intro call and cement the meeting.
1

Introduce Yourself

Start simple and human.

"Hey, this is [Name] with Rev.io. How are you today?"
2

Reason for the Call

Explain what Rev.io does for MSPs.

"I'm calling from Rev.io, and we're a PSA and invoicing platform for MSPs. We do everything from quoting, scheduling, help desk, project management, and invoicing."
3

Separation

Personalize the call before the value statement.

  • Services listed on their website
  • A specific vendor or partner logo on their website that Rev.io works with
  • Past Salesforce opportunities or notes
4

MSP Value Statement

Connect the problem to Rev.io's business outcome.

"Rev.io gives MSPs a single system that leverages native AI and connects every ticket, project, and time entry straight to invoicing, reclaiming 15+ hours per month in manual work, cutting billing errors, and turning service delivery into faster, more profitable recurring revenue."
5

Close

Offer two specific meeting options.

"Do you have [insert time 1] or [insert time 2] for an introduction call?"
6

Cementing

Confirm fit, contact details, and contract timing.

  • How many employees do you have? Minimum of 7.
  • What platform are you using today?
  • Is this your email?
  • What's a good cell phone number I can send a text reminder to?
  • Are you in a contract with your current provider? Do you know the contract end date?

MSP Objections and Associated Value Statements

Handle resistance with empathy, ask one useful question, then use the value statement that fits the objection.
Objection

"We already have a PSA."

Do not argue about their current tool.

Value Statement
"That makes sense. Most teams we work with already have a PSA. The reason they look at Rev.io is to connect the revenue side: quoting, billing, payments, and customer management as services get more complex."
Objection

"We are happy with our ticketing."

Separate ticketing from revenue operations.

Value Statement
"Good, and ticketing should work. Rev.io is valuable when the bigger opportunity is what happens after service delivery: accurate invoices, automated payments, and cleaner quote-to-cash workflows."
Objection

"We are too busy to look at this."

Tie the meeting to time savings.

Value Statement
"That is exactly why I reached out. Rev.io is built to reduce the manual work around billing, collections, and customer workflows, so the back office gets time back as the MSP grows."
Objection

"Send me information."

Do not accept the brush-off without earning one question.

Value Statement
"I can send something over. So I make it relevant, are you more focused today on service operations, billing accuracy, payment collection, or supporting new recurring services?"
Objection

"We only do managed IT."

Qualify growth plans without forcing fit.

Value Statement
"That may be a good fit for your current setup. Where Rev.io becomes useful is when MSPs add recurring services, bundled offers, usage-based billing, or payment automation and need the revenue process to scale with them."
Objection

"Switching sounds painful."

Acknowledge change management.

Value Statement
"Completely fair. The question is whether the current process is already creating pain through manual billing, disconnected tools, or missed collection opportunities. Rev.io helps teams modernize that path with a guided transition."
Objection

"We have no budget."

Move from price to operational cost.

Value Statement
"I hear you. Most teams justify the conversation by looking at total operating cost: add-on tools, manual reconciliation, delayed collections, and the cost of not being able to bill new services cleanly."
Objection

"We use separate tools for billing and payments."

Highlight consolidation and visibility.

Value Statement
"That is common. Rev.io helps MSPs bring billing, payment collection, and customer revenue visibility closer to the PSA workflow so teams are not reconciling the business across separate systems."

Billing Platform Pivots

Use these when the provider describes the current billing stack instead of naming a specific competitor.
Use When

They use a legacy telecom billing system

Best angle: reduce billing errors, manual bill-run work, and revenue leakage while modernizing customer management.

Copy-Ready Value Statement
"A lot of providers keep legacy billing systems longer than they want because switching feels painful. Rev.io is worth comparing when the current system is creating manual bill-run work, billing errors, or missed revenue from usage, tax, and recurring service gaps."
Support Points
  • Single platform for customer management and telecom billing
  • Usage rating, telecom tax, invoicing, and reporting in one workflow
  • 60%+ billing-error reduction and 15-20 hours saved monthly are strong proof points
Use When

They rely on spreadsheets or manual cleanup

Best angle: the “free” workaround is expensive once labor, errors, and missed recurring revenue are included.

Copy-Ready Value Statement
"Spreadsheets can feel free, but the real cost is the time spent reconciling usage, fixing invoices, and chasing billing gaps. Rev.io automates the billing workflow so providers can scale without adding more back-office work."
Support Points
  • Use the 15+ hours back per month proof point
  • Ask what missed usage or billing errors cost today
  • Position Rev.io as automation for repeatable billing operations
Use When

They manage billing through carrier portals

Best angle: carrier portals do not give a unified customer, contract, invoice, and reporting workflow.

Copy-Ready Value Statement
"Carrier portals can help with pieces of the process, but they usually do not give you one place to manage customers, contracts, usage, taxes, invoices, and collections. Rev.io brings that workflow together so billing is cleaner and easier to control."
Support Points
  • Ask which voice partners they work with
  • Look for multiple vendor portals and manual re-entry
  • Position Rev.io as the system of record for telecom customer billing
Use When

They built or maintain billing internally

Best angle: developer time should go toward differentiation, not maintaining telecom billing plumbing.

Copy-Ready Value Statement
"If you are maintaining billing in-house, the question is whether your team should spend developer time on bill runs, tax, rating, and invoice workflows, or on products and integrations that differentiate the business. Rev.io gives providers a dedicated telecom billing platform with AI built in."
Support Points
  • Ask if developers are maintaining the billing workflow
  • Position Rev.io as a way to reallocate technical resources
  • Connect AI-native automation to reporting, ticket-to-invoice, and billing data
Use When

They are adding services or promotions

Best angle: growth breaks manual billing when plans, promos, usage, and taxes change often.

Copy-Ready Value Statement
"When providers add new telecom services, voice partners, bundles, or promotional plans, billing complexity grows quickly. Rev.io lets teams automate bill runs and change plans or promotions on the fly without rebuilding the process every month."
Support Points
  • Ask what voice partners they work with
  • Ask how often plans, promos, or bundles change
  • Use the Utah proof point: 25% billing growth in two months

Billing Objections and Cementing Questions

Keep the call focused on fit: telecom billing volume, current system, contract timing, voice partners, and meeting logistics.
Objection

"Our billing works fine."

Test for hidden manual work.

Value Statement
"That's good to hear. The question is how much time it takes to get from usage and taxes to a clean invoice, and whether any revenue is getting missed in the process."
Objection

"We are too small."

Qualify monthly billing volume.

Value Statement
"That may be fair. As a rule of thumb, around $20K in monthly telecom billing is where the automation starts to make more sense. Are you above or below that today?"
Objection

"We already have a billing platform."

Do not attack the incumbent.

Value Statement
"Makes sense. Most providers we talk to already have something in place. The reason they look at Rev.io is usually billing errors, manual bill-run work, contract complexity, or revenue leakage as services grow."
Cementing

Use after they accept the meeting.

Confirm fit and reduce no-shows.

Questions
  • What's a ballpark number of your monthly telecom billing?
  • What system is billing your customers today?
  • Do you know your contract term with them?
  • What voice partners do you work with?
  • What's the best email to send the invite to?
  • What's a good cell phone number for a text reminder?
