Cold Call Flow

Keep this visible during calls. Follow the sequence, personalize the separation, use the MSP value statement, then close for the intro call and cement the meeting.
1

Introduce Yourself

Start simple and human.

"Hey, this is [Name] with Rev.io. How are you today?"
2

Reason for the Call

Explain what Rev.io does for MSPs.

"I'm calling from Rev.io, and we're a PSA and invoicing platform for MSPs. We do everything from quoting, scheduling, help desk, project management, and invoicing."
3

Separation

Personalize the call before the value statement.

  • Services listed on their website
  • A specific vendor or partner logo on their website that Rev.io works with
  • Past Salesforce opportunities or notes
4

MSP Value Statement

Connect the problem to Rev.io's business outcome.

"Rev.io gives MSPs a single system that leverages native AI and connects every ticket, project, and time entry straight to invoicing, reclaiming 15+ hours per month in manual work, cutting billing errors, and turning service delivery into faster, more profitable recurring revenue."
5

Close

Offer two specific meeting options.

"Do you have [insert time 1] or [insert time 2] for an introduction call?"
6

Cementing

Confirm fit, contact details, and contract timing.

  • How many employees do you have? Minimum of 7.
  • What platform are you using today?
  • Is this your email?
  • What's a good cell phone number I can send a text reminder to?
  • Are you in a contract with your current provider? Do you know the contract end date?

Core Scripts

Copy-ready language for live calls. Use the shortest version that still sounds natural.
Primary Opener

Revenue operations angle

"Rev.io gives MSPs a single system that leverages native AI and connects every ticket, project, and time entry straight to invoicing, reclaiming 15+ hours per month in manual work, cutting billing errors, and turning service delivery into faster, more profitable recurring revenue."
Business Issue

Manual handoff angle

"A lot of MSPs we speak with are not looking for another ticketing tool. They are trying to remove the manual handoffs between quoting, billing, collections, and customer management so revenue does not leak after the work is done."
Growth Trigger

Multi-service angle

"When an MSP starts selling managed IT alongside VoIP, internet, cloud, security, or usage-based services, the back office usually needs more than simple recurring invoices. Rev.io is built for that growth path."

Competitor Value Statements

"Rev.io gives MSPs a more unified way to manage customer revenue, reduce manual handoffs, and improve cash collection."

"Rev.io is built for MSPs that want service operations and revenue operations working together in one platform."

"Rev.io gives growing MSPs flexibility to support multiple service lines, contract types, and billing models without adding extra operational drag."

Objections and Associated Value Statements

Handle resistance with empathy, ask one useful question, then use the value statement that fits the objection.
Objection

"We already have a PSA."

Do not argue about their current tool.

Value Statement
"That makes sense. Most teams we work with already have a PSA. The reason they look at Rev.io is to connect the revenue side: quoting, billing, payments, and customer management as services get more complex."
Objection

"We are happy with our ticketing."

Separate ticketing from revenue operations.

Value Statement
"Good, and ticketing should work. Rev.io is valuable when the bigger opportunity is what happens after service delivery: accurate invoices, automated payments, and cleaner quote-to-cash workflows."
Objection

"We are too busy to look at this."

Tie the meeting to time savings.

Value Statement
"That is exactly why I reached out. Rev.io is built to reduce the manual work around billing, collections, and customer workflows, so the back office gets time back as the MSP grows."
Objection

"Send me information."

Do not accept the brush-off without earning one question.

Value Statement
"I can send something over. So I make it relevant, are you more focused today on service operations, billing accuracy, payment collection, or supporting new recurring services?"
Objection

"We only do managed IT."

Qualify growth plans without forcing fit.

Value Statement
"That may be a good fit for your current setup. Where Rev.io becomes useful is when MSPs add recurring services, bundled offers, usage-based billing, or payment automation and need the revenue process to scale with them."
Objection

"Switching sounds painful."

Acknowledge change management.

Value Statement
"Completely fair. The question is whether the current process is already creating pain through manual billing, disconnected tools, or missed collection opportunities. Rev.io helps teams modernize that path with a guided transition."
Objection

"We have no budget."

Move from price to operational cost.

Value Statement
"I hear you. Most teams justify the conversation by looking at total operating cost: add-on tools, manual reconciliation, delayed collections, and the cost of not being able to bill new services cleanly."
Objection

"We use separate tools for billing and payments."

Highlight consolidation and visibility.

Value Statement
"That is common. Rev.io helps MSPs bring billing, payment collection, and customer revenue visibility closer to the PSA workflow so teams are not reconciling the business across separate systems."

Close + Cementing

Once they agree to the intro call, cement the meeting while the prospect is still on the phone.
Close
"Do you have [insert time 1] or [insert time 2] for an introduction call?"
Cementing Questions
  • How many employees do you have? Minimum of 7.
  • What platform are you using today?
  • Is this your email?
  • What's a good cell phone number I can send a text reminder to?
  • Are you in a contract with your current provider?
  • Do you know what the contract end date is?
Cold call rule: stay curious longer than feels comfortable. The value statement should earn one more question, then the meeting.
