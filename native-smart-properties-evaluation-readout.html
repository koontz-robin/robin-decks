3 · Decision-based motives

The buying case is about control: fewer gates, cleaner accountability, and usable operating data.

Too many handoffs

Support, operations, purchasing, field service, and billing depend on separate tools and manual gates. Work moves, but visibility and ownership do not always move with it.

Escalations lose clarity

Native cannot easily measure a ticket from tier-one troubleshooting through field resolution—including time to dispatch, total resolution time, SLA performance, and technician effort.

Resident intake must stay easy

Residents need a no-login path from the Native website or Alarm.com experience. A custom API form or email-to-ticket flow must preserve that frictionless experience.

Inventory needs a full trail

Teams need visibility into warehouse, vehicle, reserved, ordered, installed, and sold equipment—plus actual distributor cost and a defensible audit trail.

Historical data matters

Years of Airtable structures and Freshdesk ticket history cannot simply disappear. The migration must map custom fields and preserve usable operational context.

The board needs confidence

The decision affects Native’s financial and operating plan. Leadership and investors need a concise case for value, fit, cost, risk, and the transition path.

4 · Systems Rev.io replaces

Replace Airtable and Freshdesk with one workflow from resident request through field resolution, inventory, billing, and reporting.

Airtable → Rev.io operations

Replace the custom operational “glue” with purpose-built workflows for inventory, purchasing, projects, field activity, installed assets, and billing readiness.

Freshdesk → Rev.io help desk

Replace separate help-desk tickets with Rev.io boards, queues, routing, SLAs, email-to-ticket, and a full history that stays intact when work moves to the field.

One continuous ticket

Remote support and field service work the same record instead of converting, recreating, or manually handing off Freshdesk requests.

Connected inventory + billing

Parts move from warehouse or vehicle to the customer site and invoice, eliminating Airtable exports and weekly reconciliation with accounting.

Historical data preserved

Map Airtable custom fields and migrate Freshdesk ticket history so Native keeps usable customer, service, inventory, and operational context.

Core systems stay connected

Portal.io remains available for quoting, Salesforce can remain the sales CRM, and QuickBooks Online remains the accounting ledger—integrated with Rev.io rather than acting as workflow glue.
