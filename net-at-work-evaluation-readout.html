1. The business context

“Business Performance Unleashed” needs a repeatable MSP platform.

Broad transformation portfolio

Founded in 1996, Net at Work serves 6,000+ clients across North America and positions itself around “making software, systems and people work together.”

Acquisition-led MSP growth

The MSP unit has absorbed several businesses and wants one destination, one process set, and one service model for future acquisitions—including rapid 2026 expansion.

Enterprise constraints are real

The MSP business does not fully control finance or adjacent systems. Any PSA decision must coexist with Sage Intacct, Creatio, and ChangePoint while supporting shared services.

This is not a rip-and-replace exercise. It is an MSP operating-layer decision inside a larger enterprise architecture.

2. Why change now?

Growth is exposing the cost of making a CRM behave like a PSA.

Fragmented operating model

Most teams use Creatio, one acquired business remains on ConnectWise, and legacy back-end PSAs still matter. The result is inconsistent process, reporting, and onboarding after acquisitions.

Service capacity is hard to see

Scheduling lacks skill-based assignment and a clear capacity view. Alerts arrive through SMTP and require manual merging, creating missed work and long staff hours.

Customer experience is taking the hit

SLAs are visible but not actively managed, alert behavior can reset clocks, and the portal is still limited. The team explicitly connected these gaps to upset customers.

Revenue leakage is already suspected

Microsoft CSP billing and subscription audits remain manual. The discovery team said they could “guarantee” revenue is being missed.

3. Uncovered problems

The friction runs from alert intake to invoicing.

Service operations

  • Email-based alerts from NinjaOne, Datto, and Auvik require manual handling.
  • No reliable skill-based assignment or resource-capacity view.
  • SLA behavior and missed alerts undermine response consistency.

Platform fragmentation

  • Creatio, ConnectWise, ChangePoint, and legacy PSAs divide the workflow.
  • Acquisition migrations require spreadsheet exports and manual time transfer.
  • Bi-directional ticket exchange with Creatio is a core requirement.

Revenue and customer access

  • Manual Microsoft CSP license and subscription audits create leakage risk.
  • Customers mostly submit tickets through email; a portal is considered a must-have.
  • Project, agreement, and utilization data remain tied to ChangePoint.

5. Target architecture

Let Rev.io run the MSP workflow while enterprise systems keep their jobs.

Rev.io PSA core

  • Tickets, dispatch, skill-based assignment, capacity, SLA management, assets, portal, agreements, projects, and utilization.
  • Revy-supported workflows and MSP-specific reporting.
  • A consistent operating template for newly acquired teams.

Enterprise coexistence

  • Bi-directional ticket/data exchange with Creatio.
  • Clear handoff of billing, payment, reconciliation, and financial data to Sage Intacct.
  • Plan to work alongside ChangePoint while its long-term future is decided.

Monitoring ecosystem

  • Prioritize NinjaOne integration and validate Datto/Auvik alert paths.
  • Map IT Glue, Microsoft 365, Blackpoint, SentinelOne, Cove, and Five9 touchpoints.
  • Use REST APIs and webhooks for acquisition-specific legacy requirements.

Revenue assurance

  • Automate CSP subscription and usage reconciliation.
  • Support agreement, project, and consumption-based billing logic.
  • Preserve consolidated-invoice and accounting requirements downstream.
