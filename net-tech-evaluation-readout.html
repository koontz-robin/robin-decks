3 · Decision-based motives

The evaluation hinges on project control, operational visibility, and confidence that no critical ConnectWise capability gets lost.

Project schedules do not cascade

When a client delay or resource change moves one task, downstream work must be rescheduled manually. That burden discourages teams from planning far enough ahead.

Skills + multi-person scheduling

Complex projects require matching technicians by skill, scheduling several people to the same work, and moving the whole block together across shared calendars.

Inventory lives outside the PSA

ConnectWise inventory was never adopted. SharePoint lists and custom orchestration now manage hardware and rental assets, leaving a fragmented audit trail.

Project profitability arrives late

Net Tech needs real-time material and labor cost visibility—not a post-project exercise to learn whether the work made money.

Mobile adoption is weak

Only three ConnectWise mobile licenses rotate among users and see limited use. Technicians need one practical app for schedule, notes, time, parts, signatures, and history.

Migration risk is real

Nineteen years of data and workflows create understandable caution. A feature map, roadmap transparency, proven migration tooling, and comparable references are essential.

6 · Scope + watch items

The core fit is strong. The decision plan should make four proof points explicit.

Confirmed platform strengths

  • Quote-to-cash with one-time and recurring services
  • Flexible ticket intake, boards, templates, SLA tracking, and dispatch
  • Projects built from phases, tickets, tasks, time, parts, and job costing
  • Mobile app included for standard users with offline caching
  • Assets, agreements, customer portal, ACH, and card payments

Validate in dedicated sessions

  • Complex 70–100+ task project template
  • Skills-based assignment and multi-person scheduling
  • Cascading schedule changes across dependent work
  • Hardware-rental and inventory workflow
  • ConnectWise data migration depth and sequence

Known gaps / roadmap

  • Individual project tasks are not independently technician-assignable today; ticket-based work is the current model
  • Portal permissions need greater role-level granularity
  • Customer and internal knowledge-base expansion is in development

Confidence builders

  • ConnectWise-to-Rev.io feature comparison
  • Comparable 20+ user ConnectWise migration references
  • Written treatment of gaps and roadmap timing
  • Inventory deep dive and realistic migration plan
