1. Who is Netcom?

A fast-growing enterprise connectivity provider expanding beyond voice into multi-site networks, mobility, Starlink, POTS replacement, and managed services.

Business profile

Netcom designs, deploys, and manages enterprise connectivity, voice, cellular, SD-WAN, security, mobility, hardware, and managed network solutions across nationwide deployments.

Operating model

The business runs heavily through Zoho for CRM, quoting, ticketing, and operational workflow, with QuickBooks as the financial system of record.

Why this matters

New recurring product lines — especially Starlink resale and Mixed Networks POTS replacement — require clean recurring, usage-based, and telecom-tax billing at scale.

2. Why make the change now?

Netcom has outgrown the SkySwitch reseller workflow and is intentionally pausing growth to fix operations before Starlink volume accelerates.

SkySwitch-hosted Rev.io is too constrained

The current environment creates friction around custom products/services, limits API access, and keeps non-SkySwitch billing trapped in messy workarounds.

Zoho is the front office

Andrew wants sales to quote and operate entirely in Zoho while Rev.io handles billing invisibly in the background. The integration story is therefore the decision gate.

Starlink changes the stakes

Usage can swing dramatically — for example, a customer on a 50 GB plan using 500 GB. Netcom does not want people manually reviewing usage and updating invoices.

Decision window is active

End of August is aggressive, end of September is realistic, and the August promotion creates a reason to resolve the remaining integration and pricing questions now.

Netcom
3. Uncovered problems

The current stack creates duplicate entry, billing fragmentation, tax risk, and revenue-leakage exposure as usage products scale.

Duplicate entry and fragmented customer experience

Sales reps enter orders in Zoho and SkySwitch/Rev.io, and customers can receive multiple quotes or invoices because systems are disconnected.

Usage billing is not automated

Starlink, Mixed Networks, SkySwitch, and future data products require usage ingestion, allowances, overage rules, and rebilling without manual spreadsheet review.

Zoho invoice visibility is commission-critical

Gross profit and sales commission are calculated at the invoice level in Zoho, so Netcom needs confirmation that Rev.io invoice data can sync back.

Tax remittance is not scalable

Avalara AvaTax was removed after high API costs, and remittance is currently handled manually through a CPA. Netcom acknowledged that is not sustainable.

Netcom
4. Business impact if Rev.io solves it

The business case is revenue protection: automate usage capture before Starlink growth makes manual billing expensive and risky.

$50K+monthly billing from a single near-term Starlink customer could exceed the base pricing threshold by itself, making scalable usage rating and tiered pricing the central economics conversation.

Protect usage revenue

Automated CDR/data ingestion, allowances, and overage rules reduce the risk of unbilled Starlink, POTS replacement, mobile data, voice, or other usage charges.

Reduce duplicate work

Closed-won Zoho deals creating Rev.io customers, contacts, and opportunities can remove redundant entry while keeping sales in their preferred workflow.

De-risk tax and compliance

SureTax included in Rev.io pricing plus a remittance partner path gives Netcom a more scalable alternative to manual CPA-led remittance.

Avoid building a rating engine

The lower-cost Zoho + Avalara path may look cleaner, but the open question is whether Netcom would then have to build and maintain usage logic Rev.io already supports.

ROI assumptions should be firmed up with Starlink customer forecasts, expected monthly usage files, number of billing exceptions, current manual review time, commission-reporting requirements, and final tiered pricing.

5. Requirements and systems alongside Rev.io

What Rev.io must prove before Netcom can choose value over the lower-cost Zoho + Avalara path.

Zoho-centered workflow

  • Zoho remains CRM, quoting, ticketing, and sales workspace
  • Closed-won Zoho deals create/update Rev.io accounts, contacts, and opportunities
  • Recurring and one-time opportunities handled cleanly
  • Invoice/payment visibility sync back to Zoho if feasible

Usage and billing engine

  • SkySwitch and Mixed Networks importers
  • Starlink importer to be built when data access is clear
  • FTP, API, or manual CSV upload fallback
  • Allowances, usage plans, overage rules, CDR storage, and rebilling

Finance and tax stack

  • Rev.io dedicated billing instance
  • QuickBooks integration for financial system of record
  • SureTax license included
  • Tax remittance partner introduction
  • Potential later PSA Web fit for ticketing, projects, inventory, purchasing, and assets

Known open item

Invoice data does not currently sync back to Zoho in the existing integration documentation. Rev.io is confirming feasibility with product because this directly impacts gross-profit and commission tracking.

Pricing sensitivity

Standard pricing is $1,000/month or 3% of recurring billing, with the August promotion at $750/month and no onboarding fee. Netcom needs confidence in stair-step pricing as Starlink scales quickly.
