      ### Meeting Context
      - Prospect (Andrew) operates a telecom reseller business using SkySwitch, Zoho (CRM, ticketing, quoting), and QuickBooks 
      - Current billing is fragmented across SkySwitch/Rev.io and Zoho; invoices also sync to QuickBooks  
      - Growing product lines include Starlink resale and POTS replacement via Mixed Networks, requiring scalable usage-based billing  
      - Primary goal: sales team continues working entirely in Zoho, while Rev.io operates as the billing backend invisibly 
      ### Current Pain Points
      - SkySwitch-hosted Rev.io instance limits custom product/service creation; adding non-SkySwitch items is cumbersome  
      - No automated way to track and bill for data consumption overages (especially Starlink) 
      - Avalara (AvaTax) was recently removed due to high API call costs (~$2,000/month for only 3 states); tax remittance is currently manual 
      - Zoho's recurring billing module exists but is considered immature  
      ### Usage-Based Billing (Rating Engine)
      - Rev.io has a rating engine that ingests raw usage data (CDRs) via FTP, API, or manual upload  
      - SkySwitch and Mixed Networks already have importers; a Starlink importer would need to be built  
      - Starlink usage data is currently limited; a direct SpaceX partnership is potentially in progress which could improve data access  
      - Rev.io supports allowances: included data for a flat monthly fee, with overage billed automatically via configurable usage plans  
      - Worst-case scenario is a manual monthly CSV upload; fully automated ingestion is the more likely outcome for known partners 
      ### Zoho <> Rev.io Integration
      - A Zoho integration exists in Rev.io's new interface, syncing accounts, contacts, and opportunities when a deal is marked "Closed Won" in Zoho  
      - The integration handles both recurring and one-time (e.g., hardware) opportunities 
      - Gap identified: invoice data does not currently sync back to Zoho; this is critical for the prospect because gross profit and sales commission are calculated at the invoice level in Zoho  
      - Rev.io team believes a bidirectional invoice sync is technically feasible via API and will verify with the product team 
      - QuickBooks integration is also available; the intended flow would be Zoho → Rev.io → QuickBooks 
      ### Onboarding & Implementation
      - Dedicated onboarding consultants assigned; typical cadence is 1–2 hours/week 
      - Estimated timeline: 30–60 days, leaning toward the lower end given the prospect's simple existing Rev.io footprint (~$4,000/month in current billables, few outside accounts)  
      - Prospect can begin billing new customers out of the dedicated Rev.io instance immediately, before the Zoho integration and full data migration are complete  
      - Priority order stated by Andrew: (1) Zoho-to-Rev.io integration first, (2) data migration second 
      - Existing SkySwitch data is already on Rev.io and can be copied over without a formal migration 
      ### Tax Handling
      - Rev.io includes a SureTax license in the monthly fee for telecom tax calculation  
      - Rev.io can introduce remittance partners who handle both upfront tax code mapping and ongoing filing  
      - Prospect currently handles remittance manually through their CPA; acknowledged this is not scalable 
      ### Pricing Discussed
      - Standard pricing (12-month term): $1,000/month flat or 3% of recurring billing, whichever is greater; threshold is $33,333/month in recurring revenue 
      - One-time (hardware/installation) billing does not count toward the 3% calculation  
      - Onboarding fee: $5,000 one-time (50% at signature, 50% at 90 days) 
      - Pricing tiers as billing scales (negotiable at renewal): ~2% at $100K/month, ~1.5% at $250K/month, ~1% at $666K+/month 
      - August promotion: $750/month (instead of $1,000), no onboarding fee, for the full 12-month term

      - SkySwitch white-label Rev.io has no API flexibility; this is a multi-tenancy constraint, not a deliberate restriction

      - Rev.io can automate Starlink/satellite data overage calculations and billing 
      - When customers migrate from SkySwitch reseller to Rev.io Direct, Rev.io works with SkySwitch and commissions them for the transition
