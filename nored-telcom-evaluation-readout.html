01 · Why change now

The platform decision is binary: move to Rev.io—or keep fighting the same breaks in ConnectWise.

Tax balances do not reconcile

Taxes collected on invoices do not match CSI remittances, newer services lack clear mappings, and the payable balance continues to grow.

The billing chain breaks monthly

DataGate, ConnectWise, QuickBooks Desktop, and Connect Booster create a five-plus-step close with one-way integrations and recurring exceptions.

ConnectWise is on the way out

Nored has stated it will not auto-renew. Rev.io is the only active alternative, with a late-November decision needed to protect Q2 2027.

Evaluation thesis: The winning platform must make product, tax, field, project, and accounting data flow through one database—without forcing Nored through another poorly guided migration.

02 · Current operating model

Four core systems, 7,000 SKUs, and 15 years of history leave too many places for data to drift.

Current stack

ConnectWise PSAConnectWise SellDataGateCSIQuickBooks DesktopConnect BoosterSkySwitchMicrosoft 365 Calendar

Telecom is roughly one-third of the business. The rest includes structured cabling, camera systems, internet procurement, IT, and project work ranging from $15K to $150K monthly.

Where work breaks down

  • Technicians enter field parts and labor after multi-stop jobs.
  • ConnectWise Sell templates preserve stale prices and must be rebuilt.
  • E911 is billed per seat instead of per site.
  • Invoice dates obscure when products were actually used.
  • Integrations fail monthly and force manual verification.
  • Four vendors mean four support paths—and no single owner.
Current stateDisconnected systems · manual controls · recurring exceptions
03 · Target future state

A connected quote-to-cash workflow built for telecom, service, and long-duration projects.

One product record

Maintain current catalog pricing, tax treatment, inventory, cost, and billing behavior once—then reuse it across quotes, tickets, projects, and invoices.

Field-first capture

Use barcode scanning, offline caching, geofencing, travel timers, and mobile time entry so every installed part and billable hour is captured onsite.

Connected financial flow

Move approved work into invoicing and QuickBooks Desktop without rekeying data or reconciling separate PSA and telecom databases.

Defensible taxation

Apply SureTax mappings by product, produce compliance reports, and pair with a tax partner study to resolve the growing payable discrepancy.

Progressive project billing

Trigger milestone invoices automatically or manually across nine-month construction projects shaped by GCs, trades, and porting dependencies.

Workflow automation

Train the team on core processes first, then build Paul’s target model where completing one task reliably triggers the next.

Future stateOne database · one workflow · one accountable partner

05 · Commercial framework

Package the platform around the right mix of full and mobile users.

Telecom billing

$1,500/month or 2% of recurring communications revenue, whichever is greater. At $30K–$32K MRR, the floor applies.

PSA Web users

$115/user/month list with a five-user minimum; ten users were discussed at $110/user/month list.

Mobile-only users

$30/user/month for technicians who need field workflows without a full standard license.

About $2.1K MRR at five full users plus communications billing, before any negotiated package or additional mobile users.

Payments deferred
