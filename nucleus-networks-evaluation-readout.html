1 · Executive summary

The case for change is operational; the decision is architectural.

Why change now

ConnectWise Sell ends in December 2026, PSA evaluation and migration run August 2026–July 2027, and Proposify is committed through September 10, 2027. Those clocks create a controlled opening to simplify quoting first and gate the PSA decision next.

Where value concentrates

Reduce PM report preparation, ticket triage and duplicate cleanup; create a cleaner quote-to-work thread; rationalize portal/reporting overlap; and improve usable data access for Nucleus automation.

Why proof matters

ConnectWise PSA is today’s system of record and integration hub. Business Central is Lyra’s mandated accounting truth. Canadian residency, tax, PAD, co-managed IT, reporting history, and integration behavior are decision gates—not implementation details.

2 · Current-state friction

One system of record; many compensating layers.

Projects + client reporting

Work and time live in ConnectWise, while a Lovable app produces client-facing milestone, Gantt, budget and timeline views. Two PMs each spend about eight hours weekly preparing reports; the target discussed is about two.

Ticket intelligence

Rewst, ZofiQ and custom workflows handle classification, Meraki consolidation, matching, recommendations, scripting and triage outside the PSA. Current cleanup is material but not yet time-baselined.

Quoting + procurement

Sell and Proposify overlap. D&H Canada pricing/catalog, Pax8 services, clean product descriptions, Lenovo deal registration, MSRP and quote-to-PO exceptions create workflow-specific requirements.

Portal + reporting

BrightGauge joins roughly seven feeds for monthly reports and historical snapshots; CloudRadial is the portal; Power BI and Lyra reporting serve different audiences; Lovable handles project visibility.

RMM + assets

NinjaOne now manages ~8,000 endpoints and is the primary asset source. The requirement is deeper operational automation—not merely a basic synchronization checkbox.

Finance boundary

ConnectWise creates invoices; Business Central records accounting, tax and Lyra reporting; PAD handles most collections. Ownership spans Nucleus and Lyra, with middleware/configuration details still to reconcile.

7 · Risks and unknowns

The biggest risks are knowable—and manageable early.

Business Central / Lyra is the critical path

Ownership of middleware/configuration, GL mapping, Canadian tax behavior, dimensions and consolidated reporting must be documented with Nucleus Finance and Lyra. “Integration available” is insufficient.

Roadmap dependency

Co-managed IT, ticket-level labor budgets, portal/project visibility, deeper NinjaOne actions, procurement fulfillment and some AI workflows have open or evolving status. Do not contract value against dates that are not confirmed.

Reporting continuity

BrightGauge’s scheduled reports, client mappings, source feeds, clickable detail and historical snapshots may be harder to replace than dashboards. Preserve access and parallel delivery until accepted.

Migration + adoption

Approximately 90 users, custom automation, integrations and history require phased migration, training, parallel operation and rollback. The 8,000 NinjaOne endpoints amplify validation scope.

Commercial timing

Sell ends Dec. 2026; Proposify runs to Sep. 10, 2027. Avoid stranded workflow or double-paying without a deliberate coexistence plan and confirmed termination rights.

ROI uncertainty

Only PM reporting has a quantified baseline. Current triage, automation, tool contracts, implementation, internal effort and platform costs remain data needed; defaults must not be treated as approved savings.

9 · Highest-value open questions

Inputs needed to turn the working case into a decision case.

1. Finance architecture

Who owns the Lyra middleware now? Which objects, GL mappings, dimensions, BC tax treatments, PAD postings and portfolio reports must pass—and at what reconciliation tolerance?

2. Reporting baseline

Which BrightGauge metrics, seven source feeds, mappings, recipients, snapshots and CloudRadial delivery behaviors are mandatory? What does each current platform cost?

3. Current labor

Confirm loaded PM/operations rates; observed current triage and duplicate-cleanup hours; quote assembly effort; portal/report administration; and whether released hours become capacity or avoided hires.

4. Procurement edge cases

Provide representative D&H Canada, Pax8 and Lenovo scenarios, including MSRP, catalog cleanup, bid/deal registration, EHF/warranty/service/shipping exceptions and shipment updates.

5. Migration inventory

What history, attachments, configs, workflows, reports and custom fields must move? What can archive? What downtime, parallel period, rollback and July 2027 cutover criteria are acceptable?

6. Commercial + decision control

Confirm final Rev.io, implementation, integration, training and internal costs; Sell/Proposify exit rights; decision authority; competitor/status-quo score; and which roadmap items must be contractual.
