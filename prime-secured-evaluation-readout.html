Executive summary

The opportunity is an operating model, not a software swap.

Prime Secured runs a national physical-security integration business alongside a managed IT business. The immediate evaluation is replacing ConnectWise on the security side—not automatically combining both businesses or replacing every custom tool. Rev.io should advance only if it can preserve the controls Prime needs for complex projects and accounting while reducing manual work, duplicate systems, and custom-maintenance burden.

Complex-project accounting, procurement, WIP, margin visibility, and shared-client handoffs rely on workarounds across ConnectWise, Autotask, QuickBooks Online, spreadsheets, Teams, and custom reporting.

Company context

Security and IT services, built for consequential environments.

Prime Secured publicly positions itself as a technology partner delivering commercial security and managed IT services. Its site highlights video surveillance, access control, intrusion detection, monitoring, and managed IT, with a mission to “build passionate teams that deliver peace of mind.” Discovery places the company in Elkhorn, Nebraska, serving work across the U.S., Canada, Mexico, and U.S. territories.

Primary evaluation scope

The security-integration business, described as roughly 85% of revenue, currently operates in ConnectWise. Autotask supports the managed IT business.

Operating reality

Projects range from about one month to three or four years, with greenfield and retrofit work, subcontracting, change orders, dependencies, inventory, and progressive billing.

Technical maturity

Prime reported approximately three software developers, heavy internal AI use, a data lake, and hundreds of AI-built reports and dashboards.

Why change now

Manual controls scale poorly when the business is busiest.

The record supports operational pressure but does not quantify labor or financial impact. The business case therefore remains a hypothesis until Prime supplies baselines.

WIP lives outside the system

Percentage-of-completion revenue recognition and over/under-billing calculations are maintained in spreadsheets because real project execution does not fit the prescribed ConnectWise workflow.

Cost timing can distort truth

Subcontractor classification, partial vendor invoices against lump-sum POs, and missed receiving steps can delay or misclassify project cost-to-date.

Two PSA environments

Joint security/IT clients can require manual Teams routing between ConnectWise and Autotask, increasing handoff effort and service-request risk.

Project visibility is fragmented

Prime needs original contract value, revised cost forecast, actual cost, earned revenue, billed-to-date, and margin without overwriting the baseline.

Ordering remains manual

Current distributor integrations help with costs, but complete digital PO transmission, acknowledgments, shipping status, and vendor-invoice visibility were not demonstrated.

Custom software has a carrying cost

Prime values its bespoke AI and scheduling capabilities but is explicitly considering whether developers should maintain internal systems or focus on revenue-generating work.

Desired outcomes

Make the operating record trustworthy and usable.

These outcomes translate discovery into acceptance criteria; numeric targets remain data needed.

Reliable project economics

See contract, approved changes, original and adjusted cost plan, actuals, committed/invoiced vendor cost, earned revenue, billing, and margin together.

Operational fit for team leads

Project managers, service leaders, finance, procurement, and field users can execute representative work without recreating key controls externally.

Cleaner accounting handoff

Prove how quotes, POs, receipts, vendor bills, inventory/COGS, project invoices, tax, payments, classes/entities, and corrections reach QuickBooks Online.

Controlled extensibility

Define when Prime uses native functionality, analytics, API, read-only MCP, or existing custom systems—with ownership and support boundaries.

Less duplicate administration

Reduce spreadsheet maintenance, manual Teams routing, re-keying, reconciliation, and avoidable developer support without claiming automatic headcount savings.

Phased, reversible migration

Move one business/system at a time with parallel validation, data-load comparison, reconciliation, adoption gates, and a documented fallback.

Requirements & system boundaries

Demonstrated capability is not accepted fit.

Prime’s own data, edge cases, and operators should determine each status. Roadmap statements are not treated as commitments.
WIP / earned revenue
Critical

Reproduce Prime’s percentage-of-completion calculation, original contract, approved changes, adjusted cost forecast, earned revenue, billed-to-date, and over/under billing for a real project.

Subcontractor economics
Critical

Report subcontract revenue, planned and actual subcontract cost, margin, tax/GL treatment, and the self-perform-to-subcontract change without category distortion.

Partial vendor billing / receiving
Critical

Prove a partial vendor invoice against a lump-sum PO and show when it becomes project cost, AP, inventory/COGS, WIP, and cash obligation.

AIA payment applications

Native AIA form support was not available. Define acceptable export, integration, or controlled workaround; do not assume future delivery.

QuickBooks Online boundary
Critical

Project execution
Validate

Quoting and client pricing
Validate

Data, dashboards, API & AI
Boundary

Validate schema/API coverage, rate limits, identity, permissions, audit, freshness, error handling, export, data lake ingestion, and which write actions are currently supported. Treat future MCP write capability as uncommitted.

Dual-business scope
Data needed

Immediate scope is security/ConnectWise. Combining the MSP/Autotask environment is a possible later phase, not an agreed requirement or benefit.

Tailored proof plan

Six scenarios. Prime-owned evidence.

For every scenario capture owner, sample, expected result, actual result, exception, workaround, control impact, and pass/fail decision.

Long-duration project

Load a sanitized multi-phase project with dependencies, budget, schedule shifts, change order, technician/subcontractor mix, and multi-period billing.

WIP close

Reproduce one completed month’s earned revenue and over/under-billing output; reconcile to Prime’s accepted spreadsheet and QuickBooks Online.

Lump-sum subcontract PO

Create a $500k-style single-unit PO, process a partial vendor invoice, and prove committed, invoiced, received, paid, and project-cost timing.

Quote-to-project economics

Quote self-performed labor, later subcontract part of it, preserve customer billing classification, and report planned/actual cost and margin.

Accounting exception

Run a controlled batch to QBO, force a mapping/duplicate/error case, correct it, and reconcile source, GL, tax, AR/AP, and job totals.

Architecture and operator fit

Have finance, project, procurement, field, service, and data owners test daily work; validate API/data-lake patterns and support ownership.
