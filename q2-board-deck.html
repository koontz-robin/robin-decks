Floor, Not Ceiling

Channel influence is counted when Salesforce fields show channel, partner, referral, Brook, Usman, Jake Mitchell, or adjacent partner indicators. That means these numbers are still a floor: partner-warmed inbound, co-marketing awareness, and event follow-up often land in sales or web buckets.

Action Keep the H2 intake cleanup in place: require partner/channel influence fields on opportunity creation and add a secondary influence field for warmed accounts.
