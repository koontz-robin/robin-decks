Salesforce trend readout · Jan-Aug 2026

What's working

The New Rev.io motion is showing real quality improvement: bigger wins, fewer product-gap losses, and a repeatable Tigerpaw migration path.

Average New Rev.io deal size more than doubled since January Closed-won New Rev.io Web deal size moved from a low January baseline into a much healthier Q2/Q3 run rate.
2x+average deal size expansion
Software Missing Capabilities is trending down month-over-month for New Rev.io The product-gap objection is still real, but the closed-lost reason is moving in the right direction as New Rev.io Web matures.
Down MoMNew Rev.io capability losses
33 Tigerpaw migrations closed since April Only 5 closed in Q1, so the migration motion is clearly accelerating once the team gave it focus.
33 vs 5since April / Q1
MSP partner and community traction is building Recent shows are creating stronger engagement with MSP partners and the broader community, giving the team more partner-led momentum to convert.
UpMSP community traction
Billing pipeline is trending up in June and July Moving more SDR coverage into Billing and tightening inspection is starting to show up in stronger late-Q2/Q3 pipeline creation.
UpBilling pipeline after SDR coverage shift

What's not working

The front end of the funnel is not keeping pace, and Billing is showing weakness in both pipeline creation and booked outcomes.

Marketing-sourced New Rev.io MQLs and opportunities are trending down every month Website, paid, email, SEO, and event-sourced New Rev.io volume is declining instead of feeding the improved New Rev.io win profile.
Downmarketing New Rev.io creation
Sales-sourced opportunities created dipped in July after staying fairly consistent this year Sales-created opportunity volume had been relatively steady through the year, then softened in July, creating another pressure point on top-of-funnel coverage.
July dipsales-sourced opp creation
Budgeted target products are proving difficult to sell Cyber Protect, Payments AP, and Odin all carry 2026 budgeted targets, but the sales motion is still struggling to create consistent traction across those product lines.
HardCyber Protect · Payments AP · Odin
Tradeshow opportunity conversion is lagging The shows are helping build reputation and trust in the MSP community, but that goodwill is not yet converting into enough booked revenue.
Laggingtradeshow bookings conversion
Billing client New Rev.io adoption is delayed Existing Billing clients remain a logical New Rev.io expansion path, but feature-parity gaps and related delays are slowing adoption.
DelayedBilling-to-New Rev.io adoption

Enterprise Sales

Strategic sales motion

Evaluation planning · onsite workshops · product alignment
Enterprise Discipline

Move big deals from rep-led selling to project-managed evaluation.

Every opportunity over $3,000 MRR should get a formal strategic motion: visible evaluation plan, executive alignment, technical proof, partner leverage, sandbox validation, and implementation confidence before procurement.

Proofpoints

Regional market activation: host dinners and targeted events for strategic prospects in large markets to create executive access, partner leverage, and multi-threaded momentum.

01 · Evaluation Planner

Branded GitHub evaluation hub

Create a prospect-specific planner that summarizes every step of the sales process, owners, due dates, proof points, and decision milestones.

02 · Rev.io Day

Onsite workshops and executive dinner

Schedule a Rev.io Day onsite with demo breakout sessions for each user group, then conclude with executive dinner and next-step alignment.

03 · Ecosystem Map

Map partners and allies

Document the prospect's vendor ecosystem, identify current Rev.io partners, and recruit allies who can champion the Rev.io decision.

04 · Proof Environment

Dedicated sandbox with client data

Spin up a sandbox environment using actual client data so the buyer can validate workflows, users, reporting, billing, and migration confidence.

Requirement-based project overview

Build a detailed onboarding project plan that maps major milestones, implementation phases, responsibilities, and requirement coverage.

06 · Product Alignment

Early Product involvement

Bring Product in early to scope custom development, clarify roadmap prioritization, and set expectations before the deal reaches contracting.
