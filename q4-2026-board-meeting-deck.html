Why Rev.io won
Product Proof, Partnership and a Feasible Switch
  • Field experience became personal: Mobile history, time tracking, geofencing and Revii-assisted notes mapped to Antwine’s own poor-note pain. The dispatch vision addressed technicians driving Raleigh-to-Charlotte without seeing nearby customers.
  • Billing addressed expensive manual work: Agreement components, consolidated cycles, reason codes and proration showed a credible path to reduce Jessica’s reconciliation across 130+ invoices.
  • Influence offset vendor-scale risk: Antwine called Rev.io “a big bet,” but valued a listening partner, direct product influence and the chance to help build the MSP community.

  • Competitive concerns favored Rev.io: Antwine cited Halo’s onboarding backlog, third-party implementation and UK-based support as risks in his evaluation.
