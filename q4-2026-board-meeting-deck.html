Why Rev.io won
Product Proof, Partnership and a Feasible Switch
  • Field experience became personal: Mobile history, time tracking, geofencing and Revii-assisted notes mapped to Antwine’s own poor-note pain. The dispatch vision addressed technicians driving Raleigh-to-Charlotte without seeing nearby customers.
  • Billing addressed expensive manual work: Agreement components, consolidated cycles, reason codes and proration showed a credible path to reduce Jessica’s reconciliation across 130+ invoices.
  • Influence offset vendor-scale risk: Antwine called Rev.io “a big bet,” but valued a listening partner, direct product influence and the chance to help build the MSP community.
  • Migration felt achievable: API-led customer and ticket migration, an onboarding consultant and a proposed payment-token transfer reduced the work Enitech would carry.
  • Competitive concerns favored Rev.io: Antwine cited Halo’s onboarding backlog, third-party implementation and UK-based support as risks in his evaluation.
Win story: Rev.io converted two prior closed-lost opportunities into a competitive win by rebuilding relevance through events, executive access, technical proof and migration planning.

Why Rev.io won
Speed, Data and Viable Day-to-Day Workflows
  • A meaningfully simpler experience: ConnectWise latency, slow Sell-to-Manage handoffs, poor support and unusable mobile workflows made daily work harder. John noted Rev.io’s sales demo was faster than production ConnectWise.
  • Data and dashboards solved an information gap: SQL access, visual reporting, permissions and Revii could let Ron ask questions directly while reducing John’s custom-query burden. John validated the sandbox himself.
  • Workflows fit the service business: Rev.io simplified ticket-to-project work, quote-to-delivery handoffs, agreements, recurring licenses, included labor and profitability visibility.
  • Migration enabled an operating reset: A two-step load and go-live true-up created a path to clean up years of product-catalog, ticket-classification and reporting debt.
  • Core ecosystem fit was sufficient: Ninja and Pax8 covered priority integrations; multi-location routing and report controls were explored in detail. They accepted remaining gaps rather than requiring perfection.
Win story: Renewal-timed follow-up reopened the door; direct technical validation let SIP Oasis experience the speed, usability, AI and API story before signing—turning a long nurture into a three-day close.
