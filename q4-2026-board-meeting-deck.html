ICP Closed-Lost Deep Dive

Engagement, Timing & Qualification Details

Q4 2026 Board Meeting
31 opps · $35,325 MRR
Discovery No Show
  • What happened: prospects missed scheduled discovery, repeatedly rescheduled, or stopped responding immediately after booking.
  • Representative detail: “No showed disco and did not respond”; “could never get on a call”; “will reopen if we can get back on calendar.”
  • Interpretation: this is primarily a lead-quality and meeting-confirmation issue—not a product loss.
  • Action: require stronger pre-discovery qualification, confirmation, and fast recycle/nurture rules.
27 opps · $40,202 MRR
Not the Right Time
  • What happened: year-end workload, active projects, staffing disruption, acquisitions, migration sequencing, or explicit future decision windows.
  • Representative detail: IPS paused until Q1 2027; Net Tech plans a mid-2027 migration; Main Line paused around its showroom opening.
  • Interpretation: many are deferred opportunities with identifiable re-entry dates.
  • Action: capture contract/event dates, assign dated follow-up, and separate nurture pipeline from active forecast.
10 opps · $18,040 MRR
Lack of Decision Maker Involvement
  • What happened: owner/CFO never joined, operational champion lacked authority, or the champion left during evaluation.
  • Representative detail: Xssentials never secured CFO demo; Grey Matter’s champion exited; Veritaz contact lacked billing and contract insight.
  • Interpretation: deals advanced without validated power or economic sponsorship.
  • Action: require decision-maker access before demo/war-room progression.
9 opps · $18,500 MRR
No Business Issue
  • What happened: exploratory evaluations, satisfied incumbents, recent system changes, or modernization interest without quantified pain.
  • Representative detail: Wayne Alarm was only exploring; Netcuras wanted a narrow billing fix; DivergeIT was not planning a PSA change.
  • Interpretation: interest was mistaken for purchase intent.
  • Action: require quantified business impact and a compelling event before opportunity creation.
Source: Salesforce Reason Lost Detail · Representative wording condensed for board readability
ICP Closed-Lost Deep Dive

Product Fit, Commercial & Contract Details

Q4 2026 Board Meeting
8 opps · $17,120 MRR
Software Missing Capabilities
  • Requirements: D-Tools integration/CAD/vendor pricing, CPQ images, TD Synnex/Azure reservations, SOC 2/reporting, Spanish UI, and specific RMM integrations.
  • Largest examples: Alliance Systems Integrators and Audio Video Systems at $3,950 each; Architechne at $2,700; Nashville Computer at $2,520.
  • Interpretation: losses were concentrated in a small set of explicit readiness gaps.
  • Action: map roadmap dates and qualification language to each recurring blocker.
5 opps · $8,000 MRR
In Existing Contract
  • What happened: incumbent contracts or pre-existing platform-consolidation decisions prevented near-term movement.
  • Examples: AGMN under Autotask contract into 2027; Nutmeg targeting June 2027; Compitav waiting for Syncro contract timing; Bridgepointe consolidating into NetSuite.
  • Interpretation: these were approached outside the practical switching window.
  • Action: prospect from verified contract end dates 9–12 months ahead.
4 opps · $4,000 MRR
Price
  • What happened: prospects compared the $1K minimum with incumbents around $250–$833 per month or required immediate cost savings.
  • Examples: Hometown Security at roughly $290/month; Powers Protection around $250/month; Professional AV wanted savings versus a $10K annual stack.
  • Interpretation: economic value was insufficient for smaller accounts at the current floor.
  • Action: qualify spend and value potential early; avoid discount-led pursuits without expansion economics.
3 opps · $5,500 MRR
Not Right Customer Type
  • What happened: account scale or operating model fell outside current product fit.
  • Examples: BIS required one platform for MSP and software-development workflows; NNT had only five employees and legacy pricing; LANLine was characterized as a lifestyle business.
  • Interpretation: qualification thresholds were not applied consistently before opportunity creation.
  • Action: enforce employee, workflow, economic, and change-readiness gates.
Source: Salesforce Reason Lost Detail and Missing Features fields · Representative wording condensed for board readability
