Evaluation readout · November 13, 2026

Replace Autotask friction with a connected, AI-ready operating model.

Results Technology is evaluating Rev.io to simplify quote-to-cash, restore trust in agreement profitability, and reduce the manual work required to move sales, service, billing, and accounting data across the business.

Results Technology × Rev.ioCustomer evaluation summary
01 · Why change now

After nearly seven years, Autotask is no longer keeping pace with the way Results Technology wants to operate.

Confidence is eroding

The team is roughly 80% committed to leaving Autotask. Product investment and promised AI capabilities have not met expectations.

Profitability is not trusted

About 87% of work is contracted, yet agreement profitability reporting is unreliable enough that the team built its own AI/API reporting workaround.

Manual work compounds

Quote closure, contract changes, time entry, billing review, invoice cleanup, and ticket movement all require too many clicks and workarounds.

Evaluation thesis: Rev.io must prove it can be easier than Autotask without sacrificing the control, reporting, accounting flow, or multi-ticket work patterns of a 22-person MSP.
02 · Current operating model

A capable stack held together by manual handoffs.

Current stack

Autotask · Datto RMM · NeoAgent · QuickBooks Online · Pax8 · Slide · Huntress · ConnectSecure · Scalepad · scheduling tools

The 22-person, fully remote MSP plans to run systems in parallel and retain Autotask access for about six months after transition.

Where work breaks

  • Pax8 costs and user changes require manual updates.
  • $30K proposals still go out as plain Autotask quotes.
  • Closing a quote takes five to six minutes of steps.
  • Engineers switch among several tickets in short bursts.
  • Contract lines and zero-dollar labor require monthly cleanup.
  • QuickBooks should send invoices, but Autotask does today.
03 · Target future state

One controlled flow from opportunity through service, billing, and QuickBooks.

Modern quoting

Build polished WYSIWYG proposals with live catalog cost, margin visibility, approval, e-signature, and clean conversion into delivery.

Agreement automation

Connect Pax8 pricing and provisioning to recurring agreements so user changes, partial months, and contract lines stop requiring double entry.

Technician efficiency

Make time entry, notes, ticket closure, and rapid switching across multiple tickets easier without distorting reopen or utilization metrics.

Trusted profitability

Unify labor, contract revenue, product cost, and service activity so profitability no longer depends on an internal reporting workaround.

Flexible billing

Reduce monthly line-by-line review, improve invoice design, and push invoices and payments to QuickBooks Online in real time.

AI-native operations

Use Revy for tickets, recurring tasks, dashboards, and guided workflows instead of waiting for AI features that never reach production.
