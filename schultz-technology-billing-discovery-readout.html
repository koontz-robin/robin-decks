Executive Readout
What matters

The value case is operational simplicity, not a generic platform swap.

Billing is fragmented around CoreDial/Cordial

Schultz is a SkySwitch reseller, but invoices through CoreDial/Cordial. SkySwitch service changes still require separate action, and billing remains centered in Cordial.

SMS usage is the near-term pain

Textable is new and growing. The prospect expects monthly audits to become more painful as customer adoption expands.

Accounting lacks line-item clarity

QuickBooks Online receives Authorize.net lump sums without invoice-level breakdown, creating reconciliation drag.

Compliance work is too manual

The prospect manually prepares quarterly and annual 499 filings from Cordial reports and previously had a failed Avalara services experience.

Demo posture: show Rev.io as the billing control center for CoreDial, SkySwitch, White Label, and Textable data. Keep PSA as a separate, later conversation.

Rev.io Fit
Positioning

Lead with four concrete outcomes Schultz already asked about.

One billing system

Position Rev.io as the replacement for Cordial invoicing, pulling CoreDial, SkySwitch, White Label, and Textable usage into one billing platform.

Automated usage rating

Show Textable SMS allowance and overage handling by customer, with unbillable usage reporting to catch missing setup.

QBO reconciliation

Demonstrate invoice, tax, service, and payment detail flowing into QuickBooks Online instead of lump-sum payment ambiguity.

Tax and collections control

Show 499 reporting outputs, compliance partner handoff options, anniversary billing, automated past-due notices, and internal collection tasks.

Do not overclaim provisioning: Rev.io does not provide native provisioning integrations.Use REST API/Zapier as optional automation path.
