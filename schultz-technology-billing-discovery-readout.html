2. Why Change Now? rev.io
Strategic shift

Schultz is moving from stable telecom billing to multi-source revenue operations.

More provider complexity

Schultz is already operating across CoreDial/Cordial, SkySwitch, a White Label carrier, and Textable. Each additional source increases the chance that usage, taxes, payments, or billing changes are missed.

New usage revenue

Textable SMS is new and growing. Today, Schultz expects to manually audit counts each month based on how each customer was sold.

Accounting visibility gap

Authorize.net deposits arrive in QuickBooks Online as lump sums, not line-item billing and payment detail. That keeps reconciliation dependent on manual investigation.

Compliance ownership shifted internal

Schultz is manually preparing quarterly and annual 499 filings from Cordial reports after a prior Avalara services experience did not produce the expected consolidated reporting.

The timing case: nothing has to be broken for the process to become too expensive. The risk is that the next services Schultz adds will multiply manual billing work instead of scaling cleanly.
Discovery theme: “what is the value in pulling in Rev.io from where we stand right now?”Answer: fewer manual control points as the business adds providers and usage services
3. Uncovered Problems rev.io
What we heard

The problems are specific, measurable, and tied to Schultz's current workflow.

Manual SMS usage audits

Textable usage must be checked manually by customer and compared against how the service was sold. The process is not yet fully established, but the buyer expects effort to grow with adoption.

No QBO line-item reconciliation

QuickBooks Online does not directly integrate with Cordial. Payments from Authorize.net arrive as lump sums without service, tax, invoice, or customer-level detail.

Duplicate service-change work

Service changes can require action in both SkySwitch and Cordial. Rev.io does not remove provisioning work, so the value has to come from billing, rating, reporting, and exception visibility.

Manual 499 and compliance reporting

Schultz prepares quarterly and annual 499 filings from Cordial reports. White Label traffic and tax reporting history make accurate consolidated outputs important.

Internal communication gaps

The buyer noted that billing changes can be missed when information does not move cleanly inside the organization.

Collections work remains internal

Schultz asked whether they would still be responsible for collections. The opportunity is to automate notices and tasks while Schultz keeps control over customer touchpoints.

5. Requirements & Systems rev.io
How Rev.io fits the stack

The target architecture is consolidation around billing, not a forced rip-and-replace of every system.

Rev.io replaces

  • CoreDial/Cordial as the invoice-generation platform
  • Manual Textable SMS usage audits
  • Manual billing data movement into accounting where supported
  • Manual past-due email/task creation

Rev.io integrates with

  • SkySwitch usage/CDRs
  • CoreDial usage/CDRs
  • White Label carrier usage
  • Textable SMS traffic
  • QuickBooks Online
  • Avalara or other tax/compliance partners

Systems alongside Rev.io

  • SkySwitch for service platform/provisioning
  • Authorize.net, Stripe, or Rev.io Payments decision path
  • ConnectWise for CRM/ticketing unless PSA is evaluated later
  • Compliance partner if Schultz wants filing/remittance support
Important boundary: Rev.io does not provide native provisioning integrations. Seat adds, reductions, and service changes still need operational handling in provider systems unless Schultz later builds automation through API or middleware.
