      ### Jawad's iPoint Workflow Walkthrough
      - CRM: Parent/child account structure mirrors QuickBooks hierarchy; all proposals, sales orders, invoices, and recurring billing accessible from each client file  
      - Quoting/Proposals:
        - 207 proposals organized by custom statuses (new lead, proposal generation, etc.) 
        - Proposal items pulled from Portal integration with accurate pricing; labor time auto-populates per item 
        - Proposal revisions tracked and can be promoted; multiple templates available with one-click switching  
        - Real-time margin summary (parts cost, profit margin, markup, grand total) at the bottom of every quote 
        - Key requirement stated: Ability to build quote templates (e.g., a four-camera system) for one-click addition of all items to a quote 
      - Sales Orders: Mirror proposal layout; include change orders, billing, and product sections 
      - Invoices: Synced with QuickBooks (manual push required); payment portal link sent via email; auto-marks paid and sends receipts  
      - Recurring Invoices: Rule-based auto-generation (monthly, quarterly, etc.); auto-pushes to QuickBooks on manual confirmation   
      - Items Database: Tied to Portal; auto-populates manufacturer, category, part number, and markup (set to 60%); installed price = cost + markup + labor rate  
      - Project/Work Orders: Currently not used in iPoint due to complexity; Jawad noted this is a gap they want to fill as they grow  
      - Purchase Orders: Starting to be used 
      - QuickBooks: Online version; used for AR tracking 
      ### Rev.io Platform Demo Highlights (Tony Mehner)
      - Login & Navigation: Role-based login views (salesperson, technician, procurement); left-side navigation pane 
      - CRM / Customer Dashboard:
        - Full parent/child account support; related accounts for non-billing relationships  
        - Account tagging, custom labels, and sentiment tracking for account health  
        - Centralized service history, tickets, tasks, invoices, and scheduled events per account  
      - Assets:
        - Auto-populates from items sold in Rev.io; supports configuration hierarchies (system + components) 
        - Full service history per asset or system 
        - Recurring ticket schedules (e.g., quarterly inspections) auto-create tickets without manual intervention  
      - Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Assign SLA policies per customer for response/resolution timeframes 
      - Invoicing & Billing:
        - Unified view of one-time, project, and recurring invoices per account 
        - Recurring invoicing can be fully automated or staged for review before sending 
        - Payment ledger, payment accounts, and balance overview per account 
        - Built-in collections automation: Configurable escalation timeline (email reminders, call lists, letters) for past-due accounts  
      - Recurring Services & Agreements:
        - Multiple recurring services per account, consolidated into a single invoice per billing cycle  
        - Bill profiles define payment dates; supports monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, and multi-year cycles  
        - Automated proration when changes take effect mid-cycle 
        - Agreements track labor/block time included in recurring services; overage billed automatically  
      - Quoting:
        - Revenue, cost, and margin displayed at the top; taxes auto-calculated via SureTax (included) based on service address  
        - Tax can be overridden or omitted per account (e.g., nonprofits, capital improvement projects)  
        - Portal integration syncs real-time cost pricing; also integrates with Ingram Micro and ScanSource  
        - Bulk pricing adjustment tool: apply markup/markdown to selected items and preview margin impact before applying  
        - Macros (templates): Pre-built product groupings added to quotes in one click; multiple macros can be combined; items are fully editable after adding   
        - Three pre-sales objects: Opportunities, Quotes, Proposals (client-facing) 
      - Proposal Output:
        - Full WYSIWYG editor; 26+ proposal formats; supports custom cover pages, images, video, text, and page breaks  
        - Built-in e-signature (no need for DocuSign/Adobe Sign); signing order and field assignment supported 
        - Auto-populates client name, sales order number, revision number, salesperson via variables  
        - PDF, Word, or Excel attachments can be added to individual proposals without affecting the template 
        - Quote status tracking: draft → sent → viewed → completed/declined, with notifications via text or email 
      - HubSpot Integration:
        - When a HubSpot account reaches a specified stage, it auto-creates a lead or customer in Rev.io; eliminates double entry  
        - HubSpot deals can auto-create opportunities in Rev.io 
        - Jawad confirmed HubSpot use is primarily for marketing/prospecting and email tracking; primary integration need is CRM sync to avoid duplicate data entry
