03 · Problems uncovered

Five bottlenecks define the business case.

56-click onboarding

Creating a customer and first service in NISC is a manual, high-friction process that does not scale cleanly.

Integration ceiling

NISC has delivered no new APIs in seven years, leaving only fragmented calls and manual handoffs.

QuoteWorks replacement

Silver Star wants customer-selectable packages, contract choices and digital signature—not static proposals.

Project visibility gap

Capital projects are tracked manually for financials, without real-time leadership or customer status.

Payment friction

Pre-customer design fees and open-access/wholesale charges are cumbersome when the payer is not a direct billing customer.

Outage orchestration

A single fiber cut can affect hundreds of customers with different SLAs; the mass-ticket workflow is not yet proven.

Sources: Aug. 24, Aug. 31 and Sept. 10 meeting notes.
04 · ROI & expected impact

Translate operational friction into capacity, cash flow and risk reduction.

Discovery established the scale and the bottlenecks, but not enough labor and volume data for a defensible single-dollar ROI. The model below keeps formulas visible and assumptions explicit.

Customer setup capacity

Problem
56 clicks to create a customer and first service.
Consequence
Growth consumes back-office capacity and increases handoff/error exposure.
Baseline
Data needed Monthly activations and average minutes per setup.
Impact logic
activations/mo × minutes saved × 12 ÷ 60
Expected impact
Annual labor hours released + faster activation

Project visibility

Problem
Manual capital-project tracking with no shared real-time view.
Consequence
Status chasing, delayed escalation and weak customer visibility.
Baseline
Data needed Active projects, update hours and delay/rework history.
Impact logic
projects × update hrs/mo × loaded rate × 12
Expected impact
Coordination cost removed + margin protected

Quote-to-order velocity

Problem
QuoteWorks and static workflows sit apart from operations and billing.
Consequence
Duplicate entry, slower acceptance and avoidable package errors.
Baseline
Data needed Quote volume, touches, conversion time and rework.
Impact logic
quotes/mo × admin min saved × 12 ÷ 60
Expected impact
Seller/admin capacity + shorter order cycle

Payment and billing friction

Problem
Engineering fees and wholesale charges are difficult before a full account exists.
Consequence
Cash collection is delayed or requires costly account workarounds.
Baseline
Data needed Monthly transactions, invoice lag, DSO and write-offs.
Impact logic
transactions × days accelerated × avg value
Expected impact
Earlier cash + fewer manual collection steps
