Executive summary

The case for change is operational—not cosmetic.

Slice has consensus to replace Autotask, which currently sits beside QuickBooks Desktop, spreadsheets, and a separate pre-sales CRM. The central issue is broken handoffs: upgrades and installs can miss billing, technicians complete tickets after the fact, and simple quotes take about 30 minutes. Rev.io should be judged on whether one controlled workflow closes those gaps and produces trustworthy financial outputs.
1

Why change

Manual relay between service and billing creates leakage and re-keying. Project workflows have not worked for Slice, renewal reminders are weak, and field adoption is low.

2

Why now

The team has revisited replacement attempts, completed an end-to-end demo, and moved to product/contract validation. Contract review is the recorded next step for November 10.

3

What decides fit

QuickBooks Desktop sync, quote speed, upgrade-to-billing control, field time capture, project execution, migration, renewal reminders, and reporting must pass with Slice scenarios.

Who Slice is

WiFi operations with field and financial complexity.

Slice describes itself publicly as a New York-based provider of permanent and temporary WiFi serving customers nationwide. Salesforce records 12 employees and a team supporting quoting, installs, service, recurring upgrades, and finance. Public website context is used only for this high-level description.

Current stack

Autotask for CRM, quoting, and service; QuickBooks Desktop for accounting; spreadsheets for estimates; and a separate pre-sales CRM.

Operating motion

WiFi equipment quotes, installs, service tickets, recurring bandwidth upgrades, project work, and customer contracts.

Commercial scope recorded

Eight standard users and four field users; $1,000/month plus $2,500 one-time setup and training. Commercial terms must be reconfirmed in contract review.

Problems & consequences

Value leaks at each handoff.

The discovery record supports the issues below. Dollar impact remains unquantified and is intentionally not invented.

Slow quoting

A simple Autotask quote takes about 30 minutes versus about 5 minutes in John’s spreadsheet, creating avoidable admin time and tool switching.

Missed billing

Service upgrades can fail to reach billing a few times per quarter. Past installs reportedly went unbilled for a month before process cleanup.

Delayed field records

Technicians do not use Autotask consistently in the field and complete tickets later, weakening time accuracy and install profitability visibility.

Fragmented customer lifecycle

Leads, customers, estimates, work, billing, and accounting span separate systems and manual messages.

Weak project and renewal control

Autotask project management did not work for larger projects, and contract renewal reminders are described as inadequate.

Finance confidence

The CFO wants install time tracked and is considering QuickBooks Online, making accounting boundaries and migration sequencing material.

Desired outcomes

Define success in measurable operating terms.

Targets beyond the sourced quote-time baseline require Slice confirmation.

Quote in minutes

Rebuild representative WiFi quotes with bundles, cost, margin, tax, and approval—without reverting to a spreadsheet.

Capture every approved change

Turn a bandwidth upgrade or install change into recurring or one-time billing with an auditable trail.

Field time reaches finance

Technicians record timers, parts, photos, notes, and signatures in the mobile workflow; finance sees invoice and profitability impact.

One customer lifecycle

Carry lead, quote, project, ticket, agreement, and invoice without manual re-entry.

Dependable projects and renewals

Prove phases, budgeted hours, change control, status visibility, and actionable renewal reminders.

Reconciled accounting

Sync the agreed objects to QuickBooks Desktop, handle exceptions, and produce a repeatable close process.

Tailored proof plan

Six Slice scenarios. Pass/fail evidence.

Use sanitized samples and record owner, evidence, exception, workaround, and business impact for every gate.

Quote a WiFi deployment

Build from catalog/bundles, show cost and margin, secure approval, and convert the signed quote to work.

Process a bandwidth upgrade

Move 50 Mbps to 100 Mbps, update recurring billing, invoice the right period, and prove nothing relies on an office message.

Complete a field install

Schedule, dispatch, time, parts, photos, notes, signature, completion, and invoice using a representative field user.

Run a larger project

Use phases, budgeted hours, change order, actuals, profitability, and billing through completion.

Reconcile QuickBooks

Sync a controlled batch, force a mapping/error case, correct it, prevent duplication, and reconcile totals.

Prove migration and controls

Load a representative Autotask sample, verify completeness, permissions, renewals, pinned context, and cutover ownership.

Interactive value model

Keep economics auditable.

Quote-time capacity and recovered billing are modeled separately to avoid double counting. Capacity is not automatically cash savings. Blank inputs are treated as zero.
No missed/delayed revenue is claimed until Slice supplies counts and margin. Employee time is capacity value, not cash, unless tied to overtime, contractor, or hiring avoidance.
