3 · Challenges / decision-based motives

The buying case is operational: fewer handoffs, stronger control, and a cleaner path from fieldwork to cash.

Duplicate entry across systems

Accepted work and payments must be recreated across Portal, Trello, MicroKey, inventory tools, calendars, and accounting—creating delay and misalignment.

Incomplete inventory workflow

Inflow is about 90% implemented for warehouse visibility, but parts still do not flow cleanly into projects, technician trucks, serialized assets, or customer sites.

Technician workflow gaps

Scheduling and job updates depend on Google Calendar and separate GPS data. Techs want one mobile place for tickets, time, notes, parts, signatures, and closeout.

Recurring service complexity

Five hundred accounts run on multiple cadences. The current stack limits efficient recurring inspections, bundled service plans, proration, profitability, and future maintenance packages.

Payments remain disconnected

Portal deposits flow through Square, but payments and tax handling are manually re-entered into MicroKey. On-site collections are desired but not operational today.

Seasonal deadline

Memorial Day through Labor Day is the peak season. The team needs a fully functioning system by March 1, making onboarding timing and internal adoption part of the decision.
