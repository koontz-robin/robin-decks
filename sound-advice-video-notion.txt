### Demo: Revy AI Assistant

      - Adheres to each user's security role permissions — cannot be used as a workaround 
      - Technicians expressed interest in using talk-to-text with Revy to dictate and then professionalize field notes 

### Demo: CRM & Customer Records
      - Stores contacts, addresses, notes, and account history in a traditional CRM layout 
      - Revy surfaces an account "heartbeat" summary (open tickets, billing balance, auto-pay status) without requiring manual navigation 
      - Supports parent-child account relationships, document repository, and custom fields with instant API names 
      - Pop-up account notes can be scoped to customer record, ticket, or both (e.g., "open 9–5 only") 

### Demo: Portal.io Integration
      - Rev.io syncs its product catalog with Portal; accepted Portal quotes auto-import into Rev.io with line-item hardware, rates, and costs   
      - Two integration modes: (1) Portal as the quoting system (ingesting proposals into Rev.io) or (2) Rev.io's own quoting module for smaller/one-off jobs — both included  
      - Accepted quotes automatically generate recurring service records and/or service tickets with parts pre-populated  

### Demo: Recurring Services & Agreements
      - Recurring services support any billing cadence (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual, custom) with proration handled automatically 
      - Services stay in "pending" status after quote acceptance until billing is manually activated (e.g., post-installation) 
      - Agreements module can bundle multiple recurring and one-time items (e.g., monitoring + fire inspection + battery changes + block hours) into a service package, with asset tracking and profitability visibility per agreement   
      - Anna confirmed interest in service plan packages for the future (batteries, spring fire-ups, winter teardowns, firmware updates, outdoor PV takedowns) — currently not offered due to system limitations  

### Demo: Dispatch Board & Scheduling
      - Visual dispatch board with technician groups (Tier 1, Tier 2); tickets dragged and dropped onto calendars  
      - One-to-one sync with Google Calendar or Office 365 — shows internal blocks and conflicts 
      - Dispatch map shows technician locations only when a travel or work timer is active (not continuous surveillance) 
      - Automated customer SMS when tech is within 250–500 feet of the site; push notifications prompt techs to start/stop timers 
      - GPS geo-stamps all notes and correspondence logged from the field ticket 
      - Verizon Connect: No out-of-the-box integration; open API available but would require custom work 

### Demo: Mobile App (Technician Workflow)
      - Caches daily tickets + last 3 service tickets for offline use  
      - Travel timer auto-clocks out within 250–500 feet of site; prompts tech to start work timer 
      - Techs can: update notes (with Revy AI summary), change ticket status, capture customer signatures, add parts from catalog (or scan barcodes), log time, and submit for billing    
      - "Church it up" button in Revy professionalizes informal tech notes for customer-facing records 
      - Field invoicing is available but not currently used by this team; interest in future on-site payment collection noted  
      - Clock-in/out for the day tracks daily utilization separately from job-specific time logs 

### Demo: Ticketing & Inbound Workflow
      - Tickets created via phone (manual), email-to-ticket (domain matching auto-assigns), customer portal, or API  
      - Email keywords can auto-route tickets to specific boards and ticket types; AI will make routing more intelligent over time  
      - Ticket templates available for recurring issue types; supports required fields (e.g., must assign a board)  
      - Leads can be created for new prospects calling in for service without converting them to a full customer immediately 
      - SLAs and response timers can escalate tickets automatically if response thresholds are breached 
      - Job costing visible at the ticket level (quoted vs. actual labor and material, margin)  

### Demo: Projects Module
      - Projects are made up of phases; phases contain tickets with parts and labor  
      - Budget hours per project/phase; alerts fire at configurable thresholds (e.g., 80% of budgeted hours) 
      - Gantt chart view for timeline management 
      - Flexible invoicing: deposit, milestone, final, or bill-as-you-go; tracks % billed per line item   
      - Project-level job costing aggregates all ticket margins in real time
