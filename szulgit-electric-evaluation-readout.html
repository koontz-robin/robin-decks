Connect field work, quoting, time, billing, and accounting in one operating system.

Szulgit Electric is evaluating Rev.io to replace a fragmented back office with one connected workflow for dispatch, mobile time, proposals, follow-up, invoicing, payments, and QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise.

Szulgit Electric × Rev.ioCustomer evaluation summary
01 · Why change now

Szulgit Electric has outgrown a fragmented workflow built across ZenTrades, Word, and QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise.

Too many disconnected tools

Dispatch lives in ZenTrades, proposals are built in Word, and accounting lives in QuickBooks. Each handoff adds duplicate work and weakens visibility.

Field time needs control

Eric needs mobile clock-in/out, travel and job timers, location context, missed-clock-in alerts, and administrator corrections without chasing paper timecards.

Quotes need a real process

Word proposals require manual follow-up. Szulgit wants e-signature plus automated 3-, 15-, 30-, and 45-day quote follow-up.

Evaluation thesis: Rev.io must prove it can consolidate the workflow without forcing Szulgit to compromise on mobile usability, QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise, vendor pricing, or flexible access for field and temporary workers.
02 · Current operating model

A field-service workflow split across dispatch, documents, and accounting.

Current stack

ZenTrades · Microsoft Word · QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise · mobile phones · ADI · Graybar

The desired user mix is approximately three standard users and eight field users, with occasional temporary-worker access.

Where work breaks

  • Dispatch and accounting operate in separate systems.
  • Quotes and proposals are produced manually in Word.
  • Follow-up requires repeated manual outreach.
  • Time entry needs mobile, location-aware controls.
  • Sick-call reassignments must work from a phone.
  • QuickBooks Desktop sync and check handling remain unresolved.
03 · Target future state

One controlled flow from estimate through field execution, invoicing, and QuickBooks.

Modern quoting

Build branded proposals, capture embedded e-signature, and trigger structured follow-up at 3, 15, 30, and 45 days.

Mobile field time

Capture travel and job time from the mobile app, use location context, alert on missed clock-ins, and support administrator corrections.

Dispatch resilience

Reassign work from a phone when an employee calls out, while preserving clear permissions and technician schedules.

Vendor-aware estimating

Bring ADI pricing into the workflow and confirm the path for Graybar so material cost and margin stay current.

Connected accounting

Synchronize invoices and payments with QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise and validate how checks entered in QuickBooks flow back.

Operational visibility

Use roles, dashboards, SLA tracking, technician profitability, collections workflows, and Revy to manage the operation from shared data.
