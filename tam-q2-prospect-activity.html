TAM Coverage Benchmarks

Directional account-coverage comparisons
30-Day Touch Coverage
40%-95%

Directional account coverage benchmarks vary heavily by account tier: Tier A target accounts often expect 85%-95% monthly touch coverage, Tier B 65%-80%, and Tier C 40%-60%.

Quarterly Meeting Coverage
10%-70%

Meeting coverage benchmarks also tier down: Tier A accounts are often modeled at 50%-70% quarterly meeting coverage, Tier B at 25%-45%, and Tier C at 10%-25%.

Active Buying Window
7%-8%

6sense argues that only about 7%-8% of TAM is close enough to a buying decision at any given moment to merit active rep attention, which supports prioritizing signal-rich coverage over blanket coverage.

Usable Outbound TAM
~10%

Some B2B TAM guidance warns that a 50,000-company TAM may translate into fewer than 5,000 realistically usable outbound accounts over a 12-24 month horizon once fit, data quality, and reachability are considered.

Data Coverage Floor
70%+

Modern TAM programs track whether accounts have valid emails, phone numbers, and assigned owners. One recent guide suggests triggering re-enrichment when email coverage falls below 70%.

Rev.io Q2 Coverage
13%

Rev.io touched 7,311 of 56,866 TAM accounts in Q2. That is above the broad active-buying-window estimate, but below high-priority target-account touch coverage benchmarks.

Benchmark note: public TAM coverage benchmarks are directional and usually depend on tiering. Rev.io's 13% full-TAM quarterly coverage should be judged differently for Tier A, Tier B, and Tier C accounts.
