2 · What must change

The current stack is fragmented; the new model multiplies the seams.

Uncovered pains

  • Halo, Work 365, QuickBooks, and FlexPoint divide service, billing, accounting, and payment workflows.
  • Each MSP may bring many child tenants, each with products, quantities, tax locations, and future model changes.
  • Partners need consolidated invoices with sub-tenant detail to rebill their clients cleanly.
  • Seat changes require effective dating and proration; cohorts require repeatable setup.
  • TransformITive is concerned that QuickBooks synchronization could disrupt the product database.

Required operating thread

  • HubSpot lead → quote → signed documents → tenant services → parent invoice.
  • $1,500–$2,500 monthly MSP engagement fee plus Security Assured and AI Assured tenant charges.
  • Monthly, quarterly, semiannual, annual, and multi-year cadences where required.
  • Invoice on the first; ACH/card collection on the fifth; payment status back to accounting.
  • Convert year-one per-user terms to per-tenant recurring charges without orphaning agreements or history.

Capacity

Templates, packages, quote duplication, bill profiles, and automated proration can reduce repetitive setup as partner cohorts grow.

Cash flow

Autopay and clear tenant detail may reduce disputes and collection lag. The baseline DSO and dispute rate were not measured.

Risk

Buy-now-pay-later and advance deposits up to $100K were described as important but not currently available; an acceptable interim path is unknown.

7 · Highest-value qualifying questions for Patrick

These answers materially change value, scope, or risk.

1 · Volume and growth

At launch and at 12/24 months, how many MSP partners, child tenants, and billable users do you expect—and how many changes, invoices, and payments will that create each month?

2 · Manual cost

By role, how many hours per week will partner setup, tenant changes, proration, invoicing, reconciliation, disputes, and collections take in the planned MXP process, and what is each role’s loaded cost?

3 · Revenue timing

What committed cohort launch dates and MRR are at risk because the billing stack is not ready? What would Rev.io specifically accelerate, by how many months, and at what contribution margin?

4 · Cash baseline

What are current or forecast DSO, autopay adoption, invoice-dispute rate, ACH/card mix, failure rate, write-offs, and cost of capital—and which can this workflow credibly change?

5 · Replacement boundary

Which Halo, Work 365, FlexPoint, PandaDoc, or other licenses/modules can actually be cancelled for the MXP motion, at what cost and renewal date, and which must remain?

6 · All-in investment

How many named users are required, what payment/tax/AI usage is expected, and what are implementation, migration, API, training, support, internal labor, and contingency costs?

7 · Power and process

Who is the economic buyer, technical approver, finance owner, and final signer? What budget, decision date, alternatives, must-pass criteria, and consequences of delay govern the decision?

8 · Non-negotiable gaps

Are BNPL and advance deposits up to $100K day-one gates? If yes, what volume and economics depend on them, what interim process is acceptable, and what commitment must be contractual?

9 · Accounting acceptance

What is the authoritative QuickBooks product/customer structure, and which create/update/payment/reversal scenarios must pass without duplicates or broken reporting?

10 · Scale conversion

How will a cohort’s year-one per-user agreements convert to per-tenant pricing—effective date, approvals, notice, proration, exceptions, renewals, and audit trail—and who owns that operation?

8 · Recommendation

Move forward—to a gated proof, not an assumption-led purchase.

Why advance

The demonstrated billing model maps closely to the MXP structure: parent fees, tenant products, detailed roll-up, proration, multi-location quotes, e-signature, and automation.

What must be true

The full workflow must survive TransformITive data, QuickBooks rules, payment operations, cohort conversion, API/portal expectations, and the final commercial denominator.

What not to buy on faith

Do not include Pax8 provisioning, rebuilt portal outcomes, MCP expansion, BNPL, advance deposits, or legacy PandaDoc migration in ROI until accepted or contractually controlled.

The winning case is not “one more PSA.” It is a repeatable way to turn every new MSP cohort into accurate recurring revenue and faster cash without multiplying back-office work.
