1. Who is Trigent Technologies?

A long-tenured ConnectWise MSP ready to validate a cleaner operating model.

Company profile

Trigent Technologies is a managed IT services provider serving small businesses around Bartlesville, Owasso, and nearby Oklahoma markets.

Service model

The website positions Trigent around computer repair, server management, network infrastructure, cloud solutions, and onsite assessments for small-business clients.

Decision context

Ed is the owner and decision maker. Jason from operations is a key evaluator, especially because he is pushing strongly for NinjaOne.

The core evaluation is not just PSA software. It is whether Trigent can reset PSA + RMM before the November renewal without breaking billing, technician workflows, or operational visibility.

2. Why make the change now?

The renewal window turns dissatisfaction into a real decision.

ConnectWise renewal pressure

The November renewal creates a fixed window. A decision by the end of August gives Trigent a realistic implementation runway before another ConnectWise term becomes the default path.

Ecosystem fatigue

Ed has been on ConnectWise since roughly 2010-2012. He described newer ConnectWise RMM as only “OK,” and noted that promises can only last so long.

NinjaOne creates a reset moment

If Trigent leaves ConnectWise PSA, it likely leaves ConnectWise RMM too. Rev.io can frame the future model as Rev.io PSA + NinjaOne instead of staying half-inside ConnectWise.

Commercial flexibility matters

Rev.io’s month-to-month model and $1,000/month minimum give Ed a lower-risk commercial path than a long renewal commitment.

3. Uncovered problems

The deal will be won or lost on operational confidence.

Renewal risk

  • Compressed timeline before November
  • Implementation plan must be credible by end of August
  • Decision slip risks defaulting into another ConnectWise cycle

Cordial billing dependency

  • Cordial currently pushes products, taxes, and invoice data into ConnectWise
  • Trigent bills customers from ConnectWise today
  • Exact parity is not available out of the box and needs validation

Asset tracking

  • Customer-owned equipment may be active, idle, stored, or redeployed
  • Support contracts should bill active devices only
  • Manual tracking creates billing accuracy and trust risk

Jason’s operational buy-in

  • Jason needs time to validate after vacation
  • NinjaOne expectations need to be addressed directly
  • Ticketing, RMM alerts, assets, and technician process are likely scrutiny points

Accounting caution

  • Trigent uses QuickBooks Desktop
  • The bookkeeper prefers manual entry because prior automations broke workflows
  • Batch export / controlled import should be positioned before real-time sync

Mobile limitations

  • ConnectWise mobile handles basics like tickets and notes
  • Rev.io needs to show a better onsite workflow, not just a longer feature list
  • Geofence/GPS should be job-based rather than always-on tracking

5. Requirements and systems alongside Rev.io

The Cordial workflow is the technical landmine to defuse early.

Rev.io must replace

  • ConnectWise PSA ticketing and time tracking
  • Service workflow, scheduling, and technician mobile tasks
  • Reporting, dashboards, quoting, and eSignature
  • Asset status and customer/site visibility

Systems alongside Rev.io

  • NinjaOne as likely RMM direction
  • Pax8 and Guardz commerce alignment
  • QuickBooks Desktop through controlled export/import
  • Auvik / Augment considerations as needed

Cordial validation

  • Document products / SKUs, taxes, invoice lines, customer IDs, CDRs, timing, and frequency
  • Confirm whether Rev.io Billing can become the voice billing system of record
  • Define day-one workaround: CSV, import, custom integration, or phased transition
Do not bury Cordial. Name it, validate it, and bring Ed a clear path: native Rev.io Billing, integration feasibility, manual bridge, or phased coexistence.
