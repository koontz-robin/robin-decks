Why this evaluation matters

True Blue’s customer promise is strong. The operating model behind it should be equally dependable.

Protect revenue

Reduce the risk that newly deployed services or changing quantities remain unreported or unbilled.

Return capacity

Spend less time reconciling invoices, correcting taxes, and reviewing technician entries one at a time.

Support growth

Create a modern foundation for managed IT, cybersecurity, cloud, AI enablement, Copilot, and automation services.

The decision is not simply whether to replace ConnectWise. It is whether the next platform can make billing and service delivery measurably easier to operate.

True Blue Technology × Rev.io03
Business challenges

Five friction points define the evaluation.

Revenue

Missed recurring charges

New services and agents have sometimes gone unbilled for a period, creating back-billing and leakage risk.

Billing

Quantity reconciliation

AppRiver and other variable services require detailed review against customer billing.

Service

Time-entry approvals

Remote versus on-site work, travel, notes, and time amounts can require manual correction.

Finance

Invoice and tax friction

Recurring and time-based charges need a clearer path to accurate, customer-friendly invoices.

Management

Complex reporting

The current reporting experience is cumbersome, and the platform is viewed as difficult to understand and underused.

Scale

Too many workarounds

Separate modules, middleware, and custom processes increase handoffs and administrative dependence.

Future-state design

One connected path from service activity to confident billing.

01

Capture

Standardize customer, agreement, service, ticket, and technician information.

02

Reconcile

Surface quantity changes, billing exceptions, and missing recurring charges before invoicing.

03

Approve

Focus reviewers on entries that need attention and reduce repetitive corrections.

04

Bill & learn

Create clearer invoices and use reporting to improve service and business performance.

The goal is not automation for its own sake. It is a controlled workflow that preserves True Blue’s attention to detail while reducing the cost of that control.

What Rev.io must prove

A confident decision requires workflow evidence—not promises.

AppRiver and quantity reconciliation

Validate the acceptable workflow with representative data, including how exceptions are identified and corrected.

Time-approval workflow

Prove the desired client-grouped, unapproved-only review path and required corrections.

Invoice and tax requirements

Test recurring, project, time-and-material, travel, and tax scenarios using representative invoices.

Reporting and integrations

Recreate priority reports and document accounting, RMM, security, purchasing, and remote-access dependencies.

Security and data governance

Provide the requested documentation for regulated-client review, AI data handling, retention, and recovery.

Migration and commercial plan

Align contract notice dates, implementation timing, overlap cost, training, and clear acceptance criteria.
