2. Business issue

The current stack creates duplicate work, fragmented data, and a hard ceiling on multi-location financial control.

Two platforms became permanent

D-Tools Cloud was intended to replace ConnectWise, but its accounting limitations forced Xssentials to keep both. Brian also built a custom bridge between them—adding another dependency to maintain.

Xero is the gating requirement

Location and department tags must flow to Xero so leadership can view P&Ls by Aspen, Vail, Denver, projects, and service. Without that control, consolidation is not viable.

Change orders leak revenue

The prior quoting platform lacked usable change orders, pushing teams toward spreadsheets and creating lost revenue and confusion on large projects.

Project work does not fit an MSP tool

ConnectWise works for service but is weak for multi-month AV projects, cost-based revenue recognition, AIA billing, retainage, and complex project attachments.

Field execution is cumbersome

Technicians face too many mobile clicks, limited attachment organization, and scheduling friction. Some teams use Excel for bulk technician planning.

Purchasing needs location awareness

Bulk buying is organized by vendor but must ship by location. A purchasing workflow that cannot filter and split by destination creates operational rework.

5. Decision-critical proof

Four items separate a strong product demonstration from a deployable Xssentials solution.

1. Xero integration commitment

Confirm scope, ownership, and target delivery for location and department tags. Validate the exact Xero fields and transaction objects with representative data before selection.

2. Multi-location entity model

Show how Aspen, Vail, and Denver are operationally separated inside one instance while supporting consolidated and location-specific P&Ls, projects, service, and security.

3. Location-based bulk purchasing

Prove or formally scope the ability to buy in bulk by vendor while splitting or filtering POs by destination. Today, the demonstrated bulk PO flow does not natively solve this.

4. Complex project financials

Document the approach for retainage, AIA-style billing, cost-based revenue recognition, change orders, and multi-bill-to scenarios. Any workaround should have a clear owner and operating procedure.

Selection rule: do not repeat the D-Tools experience. Roadmap confidence is useful; written scope, acceptance criteria, dates, and hands-on validation are better.

6. Business impact

The value case centers on consolidation, recovered administrative capacity, protected project revenue, and better location-level decisions.

Retire duplicate systems and custom plumbing

A simpler mobile experience, offline access, geofencing, schedule synchronization, guided workflows, and AI-assisted notes give technicians a cleaner path to complete work correctly.

Make every location accountable
